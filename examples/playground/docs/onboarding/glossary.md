# Glossary

- **authorization** — a hold on customer funds, not yet money movement.
- **capture** — booking an authorized charge into the ledger.
- **settlement** — the nightly transfer of captured funds to merchants.
- **idempotency key** — client-supplied dedupe token; see
  [idempotency](../api/idempotency.md).
- **DLQ** — dead letter queue; where webhook deliveries go to wait for
  a human ([runbook](../runbooks/dead-letter-queue.md)).
- **clawback** — recovering funds paid to a merchant in error.
