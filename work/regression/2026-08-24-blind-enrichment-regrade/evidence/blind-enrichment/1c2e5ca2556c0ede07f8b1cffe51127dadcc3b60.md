platform. Answers beginner questions: what access and prerequisites are needed
before day one, how to register a new service and pick its criticality band,
why a chosen name is irreversible, how to declare sensitive-data handling, the
commands for a first release, and how alert routing and rotation membership

duplicating resilience logic the platform already provides, over-stating
