Deprecated emergency playbook for undoing a bad networking release on workloads
still running the older per-pod approach — how to back a change out and restore
the previous version where migration has not happened yet. Answers questions
about halting an in-flight progression, restarting workloads in waves rather
than all at once, confirming recovery per service instead of trusting an
averaged health panel, and escalating rather than retrying when the revert
fails. Also records the silent skip that caused the purchase-flow outage.
Superseded by the current procedure wherever migration finished, and confusing
the two is the known transition hazard.
