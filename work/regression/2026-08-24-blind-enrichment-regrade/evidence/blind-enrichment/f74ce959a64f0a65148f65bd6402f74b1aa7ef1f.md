Step-by-step emergency playbook for undoing a bad networking release on the
current node-level proxy — what to open when a change must be backed out and
the previous version restored during a live outage. Answers questions about who
to notify first, how to halt an in-flight progression before reverting anything,
why capacity may only be surrendered one machine at a time, how to confirm
recovery without being fooled by averaged health panels, and what to do when
the revert itself misbehaves. Applies only where migration has completed; the
deprecated equivalent covers everything else, and picking the wrong one is the
central hazard.
