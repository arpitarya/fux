Defines the criticality bands every workload is assigned to and what each band
costs in process. Answers change-management and release-governance questions:
how many approvers a release needs and who may sign it off, the bake period
before a change may progress, the order in which groups of services receive a
platform change, and whether anything urgent may jump that queue. Also settles
two recurring confusions — that a band sets no resource budget, and that it is
independent of how sensitive the information a service holds is. Useful when
choosing a band for a new workload, arguing for reclassification, or explaining
why a change is slow.
