Historical architecture decision explaining why Calder Group once standardised
on a per-pod sidecar service mesh for internal service-to-service calls, and
why that approach was later reversed. Answers questions about the deprecated
networking design: how resilience policy such as retry budgets and timeouts was
centralised out of application code, why mutual TLS encryption in transit
became automatic and unmanaged by service owners, and what the per-pod RAM
footprint and added round-trip cost were. This is obsolete guidance, replaced
by the current node-level proxy decision, but it is still cited by older
incident write-ups and by the deprecated revert procedure, which is why it has
not been deleted.
