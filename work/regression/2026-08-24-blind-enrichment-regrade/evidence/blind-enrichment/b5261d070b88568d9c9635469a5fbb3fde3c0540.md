Command-line manual for the platform's operator tool — the cheat sheet for
syntax, options, subcommands and return codes. Answers usage questions: which
invocations change state versus merely report, why a scope argument is
compulsory on anything that mutates, which operations offer a rehearsal mode
and which do not, how to emit machine-readable output for scripting, and what a
refusal means when a call is rejected on policy versus rejected because someone
else has locked the fleet mid-incident. Also where the near-identical surge
arguments of the two networking revert commands are contrasted, since copying
one onto the wrong workloads is the documented hazard.
