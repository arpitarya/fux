# PRE-REGISTRATION — `rerank_weight`, the quality half

🔴 **FROZEN. Nothing below may be edited after the first number exists**, and no
bar may be added afterwards — a threshold written once a number is in hand is
not a threshold ([SR-RS](../../../records/0133_predictions.md) decision 10b).

## 1 · What this completes

[The Part A pre-registration](../2026-09-13-rerank-cost/PRE-REGISTRATION.md)
priced the feature (+15 to +19 ms p50, flat in corpus size) and **named its
quality endpoint as unbuilt rather than inventing one**. W-183 designed it,
[froze a screen that could reject it](../../proposals/quality-endpoint-for-reranking.md),
and [ran the screen](../2026-09-15-quality-endpoint-screen/VERDICT.md):

| | |
|---|---|
| `agreement` | **0.4141** over 524 screened contests |
| chance rate | 0.0748 |
| frozen band | `0.25` – `0.85` |
| verdict | ✅ **PASS** |

**So the instrument exists.** This document is what it is pointed at.

## 2 · The endpoint

**One contest** = `(query, target document, true line range)`, generated by
[`tools/quality-controls/cited_decision.py`](../../../tools/quality-controls/cited_decision.py)
from citations of the form `[SR-RS](0133_predictions.md) decision 19`:

- the **query** is the citing author's own sentence, markdown stripped;
- the **truth** is where that author put decision 19 in the target file, fixed
  long before any query existed;
- contests whose citing sentence shares more than **80 %** of its analyzed terms
  with the true passage are **excluded as quotations** — the rule is the
  proposal's and is not swept here.

| path | what a hit is | mechanisms it exercises |
|---|---|---|
| **`answer`** | the cited locator's line range **overlaps** the true decision's range | proximity reranking **and** the refer plane's rescore |
| **`ask`** | the **target document** is at rank 1 | proximity reranking **only** — `ask` never fetches |

🔴 **Never pooled.** Pooling prices two features as one, which is the defect the
Part A split exists to prevent. **A flip that appears on `answer` and not on
`ask` IS the refer-plane rescore** (W-108), and the verdict says so in those
words or it says it cannot tell.

### ⚠ The `ask` arm owes a SCREEN OF ITS OWN, and it runs first

The screen that passed measured **passage-level** agreement. The `ask` arm's
truth is a **document**, and nothing has been computed about whether the
reranker's objective picks the target document by construction. **So the same
screen is run at document level — `boost()` over each candidate document, the
same band `0.25`–`0.85`, the same chance-rate disclosure — BEFORE the `ask`
arms.** If it fails, **the `ask` arm is not run**, the verdict says the document
path is unscreened, and Part B rules on `answer` alone.

**Writing that condition here is the point.** Discovering it after an `ask`
number existed would leave a session choosing between a result it liked and a
screen it had not run.

## 3 · Arms

One version of fux, two configurations of `.fux/tune.toml`:

| arm | `[ranking] rerank_weight` |
|---|---|
| `off` | `0.0` — the shipped default |
| `on` | `1.0` — the value requested 2026-09-11 and held |

**No other key moves.** `expand_weight`, `[priority]` and every `[bm25f]` field
stay at their defaults, because a second lever makes every flip ambiguous.

## 4 · Headroom, declared before the numbers (SR-RS decision 22b)

Reported per path, per direction, as counts:

| direction | what it is |
|---|---|
| **improvement headroom** | contests **wrong in both arms** — the reranker had somewhere to go |
| **regression headroom** | contests **right in both arms** — the reranker had something to break |

🔴 **Zero in either direction is `INCONCLUSIVE` (22d), never agreement.** A
saturated endpoint measures nothing, and this one is expected to have both: the
screen's own `agreement` of 0.4141 says the objective is wrong about the truth
58.6 % of the time.

## 5 · The bar — unlowered

**[SR-RS](../../../records/0133_predictions.md) decision 19**, computed by
[`tools/quality-controls/verdict.py`](../../../tools/quality-controls/verdict.py):
the **net of flips** must clear the floor for the observed discordant count, and
**the floor of all floors is 6**. It tracks the flips, never the set size.

| outcome | what is filed |
|---|---|
| net ≥ the floor, **positive** | `PASS` on that path — proximity reranking earns its latency **there**, and the default remains **Arpit's** to move |
| net ≥ the floor, **negative** | `FAIL` — it is worse, and `rerank_weight` stays at `0.0` with a measured reason |
| net below the floor, both headrooms non-zero | `FAIL` — *no detected change*, a real negative that closes W-154 |
| either headroom zero | `INCONCLUSIVE` (22d) |

## 6 · Environment, corpus, and the label it carries

- **`fux-lab`** ([SR-WORK-ENVIRONMENTS](../../../records/0052_WORK-environments.md)
  decision 2 — every measurement filed under `work/regression/`), on a **scratch
  copy**; no frozen rung and no live checkout is written to.
- **The corpus is fux's own tree**, because that is where cited-decision
  contests exist. ⚠ **Dogfood, and the verdict says the word.** Records here are
  unusually well structured — numbered decisions, disciplined citation — and
  **no number from this run transfers to another corpus** without that corpus
  being re-screened.
- **One process per query**, cold start included. **Interleaved `off on off on`**,
  never blocked.
- **Per-contest rows under `evidence/`** — one row per contest per arm per path
  (SR-RS decision 22e). Every aggregate anybody computes later comes out of those
  rows and nothing else.
- 🔴 **No answer key is read.** The generator excludes `work/golden/` by path,
  unconditionally ([L11](../../../records/0012_LAW-11-sealed-answer-key.md)).

## 7 · Classification, decided in advance

**`informed`.** The contest generator and this document were authored by the
session that will read the results, and the corpus is that session's own
repository. ⚠ **An informed run is reclassified, not banned** — filed, cited,
and **never compared with a blind run or used to state a delta against one**.

## 8 · What this run does NOT do

- **It does not change a default.** Output is evidence; the amendment is Arpit's.
- **It does not revive the hand-graded veto instrument.** That corpus is retired
  and `PRE-REGISTRATION-TUNER` is not revivable
  ([SR-WORK-ENVIRONMENTS](../../../records/0052_WORK-environments.md)).
- **It does not run the supersession control.** Candidate A in the proposal is
  the case `COVERAGE_POWER = 2` was fitted to; it is out of scope here and is not
  scheduled.
- **It does not sweep the `80 %` quotation threshold.** Sweeping a
  pre-registered value after a number exists is the moving-threshold failure.
