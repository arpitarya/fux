What to do during a live incident to undo a bad release of the older per-copy proxy. Answers when to use this rather than restarting a single instance, roughly how many minutes it takes, and what to do when it fails instead of repeating it. Explains why a quarter of instances may be changed at once here, unlike the newer procedure where only one machine may be, and why health has to be read service by service. Applies only where migration has not happened, which is still most of the most critical group, so it is current there rather than obsolete.
