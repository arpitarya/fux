The decision that put a small proxy alongside every running copy of every service, to govern traffic between services. Explains why per-team libraries were abandoned: eleven different retry behaviours in one payment path, two of them repeating writes that were not safe to repeat. Answers questions about how retry budgets, timeouts and circuit breaking stopped being application code, why certificates rotate without anyone managing them, and what that cost in memory per copy and in added delay per request. This decision has been retired and replaced; it records what was true when it was retired, and still describes what runs anywhere the replacement has not reached.
