The platform's command-line tool and what each command does. Answers how to halt a rollout that is already moving, how to see which release each group is running, how to undo one, and how to find out what kind of data a service has declared. Explains what each exit status means, and in particular how to tell being turned down because you may not do something from being turned down because somebody else is already dealing with an incident and you should find out who. Warns that the two undo commands take different surge settings and copying one onto the other is dangerous.
