Obsolete but still-needed emergency procedure for undoing a bad release of the
per-pod proxy, applying only where migration to the newer architecture has not
happened — which through 2026 is most of the revenue-path estate. Answers how to
revert, what symptoms justify it, why a single unhealthy replica should be
restarted rather than triggering a fleet action, and why the concurrency option
here is a percentage rather than one at a time. Covers announcement, halting
progression, per-service recovery checks, resuming, and escalating instead of
retrying. Names the silent skip hazard that later caused the purchase-flow outage,
and warns against confusing it with the current procedure.
