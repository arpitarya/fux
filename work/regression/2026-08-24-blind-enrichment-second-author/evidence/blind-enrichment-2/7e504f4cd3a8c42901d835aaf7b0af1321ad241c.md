Defines the criticality bands every workload must be assigned to and what each
band buys. Answers questions about release sequencing and promotion order, how
long a change must bake before advancing, approval and sign-off requirements,
alert-notification behaviour by band, and how to move a service between bands.
Explains why there is no expedite path and why reducing a service's protections
takes effect immediately. Useful for change-management, gating, risk-classification
and business-criticality questions, and for confirming that the band decides
neither resource budget nor data sensitivity. Relevant when asking which category
the revenue-critical purchase flow belongs to.
