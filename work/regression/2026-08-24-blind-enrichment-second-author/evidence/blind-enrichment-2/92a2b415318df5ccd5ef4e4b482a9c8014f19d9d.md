The binding rule set for data governance and lifecycle. Answers questions about
how long information may be kept, what sensitivity band applies, what happens when
a team fails to declare one, encryption and key-management obligations, and how
erasure requests must propagate through every copy including replicas, caches,
indexes, warehouses and backups. Covers personally identifiable information
leaking into logging and telemetry, why redaction after the fact does not
reclassify a store, and why the backup window bounds the deletion timeline. Useful
for compliance, audit, privacy, storage duration, purge and expiry questions, and
for confirming that criticality band and data sensitivity are separate concepts.
