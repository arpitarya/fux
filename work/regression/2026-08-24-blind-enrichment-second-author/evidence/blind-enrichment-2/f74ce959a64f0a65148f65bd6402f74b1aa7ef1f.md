Step-by-step emergency procedure for undoing a bad release of the current
node-local proxy and restoring the previously known-good version. Answers
questions about how to revert, what symptoms justify it, why one unhealthy machine
should be drained instead, why the operation is scoped rather than fleet-wide, and
why the concurrency option cannot be raised. Covers announcement and
acknowledgement, halting progression, checking recovery machine by machine rather
than in aggregate, resuming, and escalating instead of retrying a failed attempt.
Applies only where migration to the current architecture is complete; the older
procedure covers everything else, and choosing wrongly is itself a hazard.
