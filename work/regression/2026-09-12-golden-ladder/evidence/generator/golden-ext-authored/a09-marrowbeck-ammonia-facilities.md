# Facilities and ammonia plant maintenance — Marrowbeck

## 1. The defrost window

1.1. The daily defrost check runs at a fixed time and is **not** moved to suit a
dock booking. If the two collide, the booking moves. Facilities does not
negotiate this at the dock; the site manager decides it the day before or not
at all.

1.2. Bhopal runs 13:05 to 13:40. Indore runs 11:20 to 11:50. The two differ
because the Indore plant is smaller and the cross-dock has no storage.

## 3. Work orders

3.1. A work order on any asset that holds a cold room in band is a **priority
order** and is assigned the day it is raised.

3.2. An unassigned priority order older than seven days escalates automatically
to the site manager. This rule exists because a condenser fan sat unassigned for
twelve days at another operator's site and the room only failed when the weather
changed.

## 4. Permits

Hot work, confined space and work at height each need a permit signed **before**
the work starts. A permit signed afterwards is an incident, not a permit.

## 5. Ammonia release

5.1. Evacuate to the upwind muster point. Do not re-enter to fetch records,
laptops or stock.

5.2. The plant room is entered after a release only by the external contractor
with breathing apparatus. No exception exists for "just checking a valve".
