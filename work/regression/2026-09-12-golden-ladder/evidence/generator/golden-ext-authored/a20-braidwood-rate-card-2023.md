# Rate card and surcharges, 2023

Author: Sneha Barve

Applies to standard customer quotations unless a signed annex says otherwise.

## Base rates

| service | 2021 rate | 2023 rate | unit |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Vaccine or insulin, Kanpur to Prayagraj lane | Rs. 16.20 | Rs. 19.40 | per kg |
| Dairy crates inside Kanpur city | Rs. 2.60 | Rs. 3.15 | per crate |
| Frozen intercity pallet | Rs. 1,780 | Rs. 2,050 | per pallet |
| Emergency dry-ice packing | Rs. 33 | Rs. 41 | per kg |

## Surcharges

| item | 2021 rule | 2023 rule |
|---|---|---|
| Diesel surcharge | 4.00% when HSD exceeds Rs. 82/litre | 5.50% when HSD exceeds Rs. 89/litre |
| After-hours dock wait | first 60 minutes free, then Rs. 450/hour | first 45 minutes free, then Rs. 540/hour |
| Audit packet | included for vaccines | included for vaccines |

GST at 18% is charged on freight, surcharge and wait time unless the customer
holds a written exemption letter.

* Footnote A: A weekend collection charge was discussed and not introduced on
  this card. Quote it only if the annex names it.
* Footnote B: Two customers hold annex pricing. Check with Finance before
  quoting.

> **Retired.** Kept because contracts signed in 2023 and 2024 quote these
> numbers, and because Finance is asked every audit what the rate was at the
> time. It is not a current price.
