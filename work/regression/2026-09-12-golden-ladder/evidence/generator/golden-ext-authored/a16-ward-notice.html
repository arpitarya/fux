Ward 27 — public notice

Issued 14 June 2025 by the ward office.

Water supply

Supply will be interrupted between 10:00 and 16:00 on 21 and 22 June for pipeline work near the market junction. Storage is advised. Tanker requests are taken at the ward office and not on the helpline.

Solid waste

Wet and dry waste are collected on alternate days. Garden waste in bulk is collected on the first Saturday of the month, from the collection point only. Construction debris is not collected and must be removed by the owner.

Street lighting

Twenty-two poles on the east link road have been converted to LED. Faults may be reported with the pole number, which is painted at eye height.

Draft development plan

Written objections to the draft development plan are received at the ward office for thirty days from the date of this notice. Objections by email are accepted only in the prescribed format, which is available at the counter.

Monsoon preparedness

Desilting of the main storm drains is complete. Residents are asked not to dump waste into the open drains along the service lane; the drain was blocked at two points last year and the flooding was avoidable.
