# System access — joiner, mover, leaver

## Joiner

Access is requested by the hiring manager and approved by the system owner.
Nobody gets an account on a verbal request, **including on the first day of
peak season**. The service desk keeps the refusals; they are the evidence that
the control works.

## Mover

A role change removes every old entitlement **before** adding the new one. This
is the step that gets skipped, and it is why the last access review found 37
accounts with warehouse rights nobody could explain.

## Leaver

Accounts are disabled on the last working day, not at the end of the notice
period. Shared guard-cabin tablets are re-paired the same week, because a shared
device keeps a session alive long after the person has gone.

## Privileged access

| system | who may hold admin | review |
|---|---|---|
| Telematics platform | two named IT staff | quarterly |
| Slot calendar | one named operations lead | quarterly |
| Billing | Finance systems owner | half-yearly |

**Nobody in a site role changes a sensor threshold.** Threshold changes are
raised jointly by Quality and IT, and the platform's audit log is exported
monthly and kept.

## Review

The full access review runs twice a year. A manager who does not respond within
fourteen days has the access removed, not extended.
