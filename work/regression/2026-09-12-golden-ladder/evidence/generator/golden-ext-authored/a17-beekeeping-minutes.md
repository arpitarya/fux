# Beekeeping association — minutes of the spring meeting

*5 April 2025. Present: 19 members. Apologies from four.*

## 1. Winter losses

Losses across the association ran at 17% this winter, close to the ten-year
average. Two members reported isolation starvation in colonies that had ample
stores, but not where the cluster had moved to. The usual advice was repeated:
stores above the cluster, not beside it.

## 2. Apiary site

The fence on the north boundary needs replacing before the swarm season. A
quote has been obtained and will be circulated. The landowner has agreed to the
work provided the gate position does not change.

## 3. Swarm collection

The swarm list is open for the season. Members on the list are asked to keep a
spare nucleus box ready and to answer the phone; last year three calls went to
pest control because nobody on the list picked up.

## 4. Disease

The inspector's visit is expected in May. Members are reminded that comb older
than three years should be rotated out and that a shared hive tool is a shared
infection.

## 5. Honey show

The show rules are unchanged except for the clear-honey class, where the jar
weight tolerance has been tightened. Entries close a fortnight before.

## 6. Any other business

The library has four new titles. The treasurer reported a balance sufficient
for the fence and no more, so the equipment purchase is deferred.

**Next meeting:** talk on queen rearing, followed by the show rules in detail.
