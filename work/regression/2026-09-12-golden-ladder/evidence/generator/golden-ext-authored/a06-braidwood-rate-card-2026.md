# Rate card and surcharges, 2026

Author: Sneha Barve

This card replaces the 2023 card and both of its footnotes. Applies to standard
customer quotations unless a signed annex says otherwise.

## Base rates

| service | rate from 1 April 2026 | unit |
|---|---:|---|
| Vaccine or insulin, Kanpur to Prayagraj lane | Rs. 24.80 | per kg |
| Dairy crates inside Kanpur city | Rs. 4.10 | per crate |
| Frozen intercity pallet | Rs. 2,680 | per pallet |
| Emergency dry-ice packing | Rs. 52 | per kg |
| Passive shipper hire, pharma | Rs. 1,320 | per shipper per trip |
| Cross-dock handling, no storage | Rs. 480 | per pallet |

## Surcharges

| item | rule from 1 April 2026 |
|---|---|
| Diesel surcharge | 8.00% when HSD exceeds Rs. 95/litre |
| After-hours dock wait | first 30 minutes free, then Rs. 720/hour |
| Audit packet | included for vaccines and insulin |
| Monsoon rain-cover handling | Rs. 310 per trip, June to September |
| Weekend collection | Rs. 1,100 flat, Saturdays only; no Sunday collection |

GST at 18% is charged on freight, surcharge and wait time unless the customer
holds a written exemption letter.

## Notes

* The 2023 card's footnote about a 5.50% diesel surcharge was never applied
  after 1 April 2026. Quote 8.00%.
* Dry-ice packing moved from Rs. 41 to Rs. 52 per kg because the Kanpur
  supplier revised its slab in February 2026.
* Two customers hold annex pricing and are not quoted from this card. Finance
  holds the list; do not guess from the customer name.
