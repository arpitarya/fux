# Fleet workshop and preventive maintenance — Okapi

## Preventive schedule

| item | interval |
|---|---|
| Reefer unit service | 900 running hours |
| Reefer unit belt and fan check | 300 running hours |
| Brake inspection | 10 weeks |
| Tyre rotation | 18,000 km |
| Door seal and curtain check | monthly |
| Logger mount and cable check | with every reefer service |

Running hours come from the unit, not from the odometer. A unit idling on a
yard accumulates hours the odometer never sees, and that is exactly the unit
that fails on a warm night.

## Breakdowns

A unit that fails twice in one quarter is taken off patient-critical work until
the root cause is closed. Fleet keeps that list, and it is **not negotiable at
the dock** at 22:00 by a shift lead with a load to move.

## Parts

Genuine parts for the reefer unit. The warranty is void otherwise, and the
saving is smaller than one rejected pharma load.

## Records

Every job card is closed with the running hours, the parts used and the
technician's name. A job card closed with "checked, OK" and nothing else is
returned to the technician.
