# Leave and attendance policy — Petrichor

## Entitlement

Earned leave accrues at 21 days a year and carries over to a maximum of 45
days. Casual leave is 8 days and does not carry over. Sick leave is 12 days and
needs a certificate beyond two consecutive days.

## Applying

Apply 10 days ahead for anything longer than two days. **Shift staff apply
through the roster, not by message** — the roster is what the site manager reads
at handover, and a message in a group is not an application.

## Peak season

Leave during the vaccine and dairy peak months is capped at one person per shift
per site. The cap is published in March so it is not a surprise in June.

## Unplanned absence

Call the shift lead before the shift starts. An absence reported after the
shift has started is unplanned and is reviewed after the third occurrence in a
quarter.

## Night shift

A night-shift worker who has worked a full night is not rostered onto the
following day shift. This is not a courtesy; it is the same reasoning as the
driver hours rule, and it is not waived for a slot.
