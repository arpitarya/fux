# Customer notification matrix 2026

This matrix says **who is told, how fast, and by which channel**. It does not
decide whether an excursion occurred; only Quality does that, and a notification
deadline never starts before Quality confirms.

## 1. Deadlines

| customer class | trigger | notify within | channel |
|---|---|---:|---|
| patient critical | confirmed excursion | 90 minutes | phone, then written inside 4 h |
| patient critical | suspected, not yet confirmed | 6 hours | written |
| dairy | visible spoilage or odour | 3 hours | phone |
| dairy | confirmed excursion, no spoilage | next scheduled check-in | written |
| frozen restaurant chain | confirmed excursion | end of business day | phone, then written |
| frozen restaurant chain | vehicle still on the road | immediately | phone to the receiving kitchen |
| dry grocery | any | next working day | written |

Ninety minutes for patient-critical stock is shorter than the 2024 matrix
allowed. It was shortened after a customer learned of an excursion from its own
logger before we called.

## 2. Wording

2.1. The first notice says *"temperature excursion under investigation"* and
nothing further.

2.2. Nobody promises release, replacement credit, insurance recovery or a root
cause in the first notice. A promise made in the first hour is the one quoted
back in the claim.

2.3. A notice is never sent in a chat group, whatever the customer's habit.

## 3. Signatures

| notice | signed by |
|---|---|
| patient critical, first | Fiona D'Souza or the Quality head |
| patient critical, follow-up | Quality head only |
| dairy and frozen | Abhijit Mukherjee |
| dry grocery | any customer service lead |

The shift that found the event never signs its own notice.

## 4. Escalation inside Sundari Fresh

Quality, the site manager, Fleet Control when a vehicle is involved, and
Finance when stock is likely to be destroyed. For Kochi the out-of-hours number
reaches the site manager directly; for Tiruchirappalli it reaches the gate,
which is not the same thing and has cost us an hour before.

## 5. What this matrix does not do

5.1. It sets no temperature limit and no staging allowance.

5.2. It does not authorise a disposition. A customer being notified quickly is
not a customer agreeing to anything.
