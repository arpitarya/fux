# Ammonia plant at Nagpur: levels, places and kit

## Alarm levels

- about 5 ppm — the smell is noticeable; not an alarm.
- Stage 1 — 25 ppm at any plant-room detector: amber beacon, the plant crew investigate, and dock 5 stops loading.
- Stage 2 — 150 ppm at any detector: site siren and full evacuation to M2.
- 300 ppm — immediately dangerous to life or health; nobody without a breathing set is anywhere near it.

## Places

- **M1** — canteen lawn; fire muster only.
- **M2** — beside the windsock at gate 2; ammonia and fire muster.
- **windsocks** — one on the compressor house roof, one at gate 2.

## Kit

| item | detail |
|---|---|
| breathing sets | 4, in the plant room, for plant crew only |
| eyewash stations | dock 5 and the plant room door |
| plant charge | 1,800 kg of ammonia |

## Checks on the kit

The plant crew check each breathing set on the first day of the month and sign
the tag. Four tags hang in a row by the plant room door. A set whose cylinder
reads below full is swapped the same day. The room itself is inspected by the
crew lead before every Stage 1 test.
