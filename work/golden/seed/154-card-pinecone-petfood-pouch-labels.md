# Pinecone fresh pet food: what the pouch and the carton must show

At the labelling bench for the chilled fresh pet food we pack and deliver for Pinecone.

## The pouch

Each pouch arrives from Pinecone already printed with the recipe, the ingredients and the feeding guide. We add only the date sticker: the use-by date in characters at least 3 mm high, black on white, on the back of the pouch below the feeding guide.

## The carton

Pouches go out twelve to a carton. Each carton carries one sscc label printed at the end of the bench, so that it can be traced through the van and back. The label follows the standard used by Pinecone's retailers, and scans on their tills as well as ours.

## The retailer copy

Retailers that stock Pinecone in their chillers ask for a second label on the carton's end, with Pinecone's gs1 company prefix and the product's item number, so that their staff can shelve it without opening the carton.

## Checks

The bench lead scans the first and the last carton of every batch. A label that does not scan is reprinted; a whole batch that does not scan stops the bench.

## Spares

Label rolls and ribbons are kept in the cabinet under the bench. The bench lead orders more when two rolls are left, so that the bench never runs out halfway through a batch.
