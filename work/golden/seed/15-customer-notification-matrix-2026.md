# Customer Notification Matrix 2026

Who tells the customer, how fast, and in what words, after QA confirms a
temperature excursion. **This matrix amends clause 5.1 and clause 5.2 of
QCL-QA-SOP-17 with effect from 1 July 2026.** Where the SOP still prints the
older durations, this document is the one to follow.

## 1. Timing by product class

| product class | notify within | who sends | channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pharma critical (vaccines, insulin, customer-named patient critical) | 90 minutes of confirmation | Customer Service, cleared by QA | phone then email |
| Dairy, spoilage visible or above +8.0 C | 3 hours of confirmation | Customer Service | phone then email |
| Dairy, no visible spoilage | next scheduled check-in | Customer Service | email |
| Frozen food, restaurant chains | end of the business day of confirmation | Customer Service | email, plus Fleet Control call to the kitchen if the vehicle is on the road |

The pharma window moved from 2 hours to 90 minutes, and the dairy visible-spoilage
window from 4 hours to 3 hours, after the BharatVac account review of May 2026.

## 2. Wording

2.1. The first notice says "temperature excursion under investigation" and
nothing more definite. No release, replacement credit, or insurance recovery may
be promised in the first notice.

2.2. The second notice, sent after disposition, names the disposition in the
four words the SOP uses: release, conditional release, hold pending external
decision, or destroy.

2.3. New in 2026: a holding notice is sent at the 24-hour mark if the
investigation is still open, even when there is nothing new to report.

## 3. Who may sign

3.1. Pharma critical notices are signed by the QA head or the nominated deputy.

3.2. Dairy and frozen notices are signed by the Customer Service lead.

3.3. No DC manager signs a customer notice.

## 4. Escalation if the customer cannot be reached

4.1. Two phone attempts twenty minutes apart, then the contract escalation
contact, then the account manager.

4.2. Record every attempt in QCL-F-17. An unanswered phone is not a notice.
