# PROJ-123: Dockwise rollout to Guwahati and Coimbatore

**PROJ-123** took the Dockwise slot calendar from Nagpur only to all three
distribution centres. It is closed. The project numbers in this series are close
together and are not interchangeable: PROJ-123 is the Dockwise rollout,
[PROJ-124](20-project-PROJ-124-red-sleeve-and-passive-shippers.md) is the red
sleeve and passive shipper work, and
[PROJ-125](21-project-PROJ-125-kalpa-decommission.md) is the Kalpa
decommission. Quoting the wrong number on a finance line has already caused one
re-posting.

## Why it was opened

Before PROJ-123, Guwahati assigned night slots by phone to Riniki Bora and
Coimbatore ran on security-desk calls. Neither site had a calendar an auditor
could read, and neither could be reconciled against a gate register without
someone's memory. The Nagpur page everyone actually read was an exported wiki
copy — [the old dock scheduling wiki export](09-dock-scheduling-wiki-export.html)
— whose Kalpa slot sync link had been dead since the vendor migration.

## What was delivered

- Dockwise go-live at Guwahati DC and Coimbatore DC on **1 February 2026**, both
  sites on the same day.
- A written booking rule to replace the wiki page:
  [Dock scheduling rules 2026](13-dock-scheduling-rules-2026.md), effective
  9 March 2026. That document, not this one, carries the cut-off times, the dock
  numbers and the grace table.
- Dock 6 at Nagpur brought into the calendar for dry grocery.
- Phone booking retained at Guwahati as a documented fallback for VPN outages,
  rather than left as an undocumented habit.

## Budget

| line | figure |
|---|---|
| approved budget | Rs. 6.40 lakh |
| actual spend | Rs. 7.05 lakh |

Finance approved the overrun on 12 February 2026. No recurring charge was added;
Dockwise licensing was already company-wide.

## What PROJ-123 did not fix

The new joiner FAQ still carries an open TODO asking for a Dockwise link that
works outside the office VPN — see
[the new joiner FAQ](10-new-joiner-faq.md). PROJ-123 closed without solving it,
because the answer is a network change and not a dock change. The residue is
assigned to the IT Service Desk with a due date of 30 September 2026 and it is
the only item this project handed on.

PROJ-123 changed no temperature rule, no grace period on its own authority and
no driver hours rule. A dock booking never overrules a temperature limit.
