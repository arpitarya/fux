# Hinjewadi distributor store: when a maker withdraws a batch

For the store we run in Hinjewadi for a pharmaceutical distributor that supplies chemists across west Pune.

## The instruction

The distributor's quality manager tells us in writing when a maker has withdrawn a batch. The store manager confirms by return e-mail and stops picking that batch at once.

## Where it goes

Units still on our shelves, and recalled units that chemists send back, go to the taped floor area beside the store office, marked off with red tape and a sign. The segregation is checked by the store manager at the start of every shift.

## Counting

The units in the taped area are counted once a week, on Monday morning, and the count is e-mailed to the distributor's quality manager.

## Waiting for a decision

If the distributor has not said what to do with the units 60 days after the first ones arrived, the store manager writes to the distributor's operations head and asks for a decision.

## What we do not do

We do not tell chemists why a batch was withdrawn, and we do not destroy or return any unit without the distributor's written instruction.

## Paperwork

The instruction, the weekly counts and every return note from a chemist are kept together in the withdrawal folder for that batch, and the folder is kept for five years after the last unit leaves the store.
