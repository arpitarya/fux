# Kharadi e-pharmacy dark store: taking back a customer's order

For the pickers and riders at the dark store we run in Kharadi for an online pharmacy. Medicines bought online come back here when a patient refuses delivery or the pharmacy cancels an order.

## Before anything comes back

The online pharmacy's care desk raises a return in its own system and sends us the return number by e-mail. The number is the rma for that order, and it is printed on the rider's run sheet next to the address.

## How long the number lasts

A return number at Kharadi lasts 7 days from the day the care desk raises it. A parcel that reaches the dark store after that is not put back on the shelf; it goes in the grey tote by the pharmacist's desk until the care desk confirms.

## Who decides

Only the store pharmacist may put a returned medicine back into saleable stock, and only if its seal is unbroken and it needs no refrigeration. Every chilled medicine that comes back is set aside for the pharmacy's own decision, whatever its condition.

## Without a number

A parcel brought back by a rider without any authorisation from the care desk is accepted, logged on the returns sheet with the rider's name and put in the grey tote. The rider is not blamed; the care desk is asked why the number was missing.

## Records

The returns sheet is scanned at the end of each day and sent to the care desk. Paper sheets are kept in the pharmacist's drawer for a month.
