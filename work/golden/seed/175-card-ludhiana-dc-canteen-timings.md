# Ludhiana DC canteen timings and menu days

Pinned at the canteen counter and in the drivers' rest room at Ludhiana DC.

## Canteen timings

| meal | timing |
|---|---|
| breakfast | 07:00 to 09:00 |
| lunch | 12:30 to 14:30 |
| evening tea and snacks | 17:00 to 17:45 |
| dinner | 19:30 to 21:30 |

Tea and water are available at the counter all day.

## Menu days

Monday rajma chawal, Tuesday kadhi pakora, Wednesday chole bhature, Thursday paneer with dal, Friday mixed vegetable with dal makhani, Saturday the cook's choice. Meals are vegetarian; eggs are served at breakfast on request.

## Who may eat

Staff, agency staff and contractors' workers on site eat free. Visiting drivers may buy a meal token at the gate cabin for Rs. 40.

## Complaints

Complaints about the food go to HR in writing, not to the cook.
