# Lane qualification LQ-56

Record **LQ-56** qualifies the hill dairy lane from Guwahati DC up to customer depots in Shillong.

## 1. Lane

| field | value |
|---|---|
| origin | Guwahati DC |
| destination | Shillong customer depots |
| route | via Jorabat, Nongpoh and Umiam |
| distance | 103 km |
| product class | dairy, milk and paneer |
| vehicle class | owned_reefer_chilled |
| season profile | monsoon, June to September |
| trial runs | 3, from 3 to 17 August 2026 |

## 2. Score

2.1. LQ-56 scores an LRS of 15 of 25. The climb after Jorabat, the landslide holds near Nongpoh and the dead zone above Umiam each scored 4.

2.2. The short distance does not make it an easy lane: run 2 took 6 hours 40 minutes for 103 km.

## 3. Trial results

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| warmest milk reading | +3.6 C, run 2, during a 2-hour landslide hold near Nongpoh |
| longest telemetry gap | 11 minutes, above Umiam |
| door openings per run | 5, multi-drop |

## 4. Conditions

4.1. The ghat-road speed limit in clause 4.1 of the Driver Hours and Road Safety Policy applies from Jorabat to Shillong, loaded or empty.

4.2. If a landslide closes the road, the vehicle waits at the Nongpoh police post or returns to Guwahati, on Fleet Control's call. No other route is taken.

4.3. Every run carries a USB logger beside the product. Gaps above Umiam are written as missing.

4.4. Rain cover goes over the rear pallets at every Shillong drop.

4.5. LQ-56 is valid for the 2026 and 2027 monsoon seasons unless the route changes.
