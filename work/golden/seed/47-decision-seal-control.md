# Seals: why bolt seals, number order and pallet labels

## Why bolt seals on owned reefers

In 2024 a plastic seal on a Mangal Foods load was cut and replaced with a look-alike carrying the same number, and the shortage claim could not be defended.
A bolt seal needs a cutter to remove and cannot be pushed back together. A bolt seal costs Rs. 38 against Rs. 9 for the plastic seal it replaced.

## Why cable seals on hired trucks

Hired trucks arrive with every kind of hasp, and a bolt seal does not fit half
of them. A cable seal fits any hasp.

## Why number order

Taking seals strictly in number order is what lets an auditor see a gap. A seal
taken from the middle of a book hides a missing one.

## Why pallet labels on pharma

The BharatVac audit of February 2026 asked for evidence at pallet level, not
only at the door. A tamper label across the stretch wrap shows whether a pallet
was opened between our gate and the customer's.

## What the numbers cost

Plastic seals were bought in bags of 500. The Mangal Foods claim was worth
Rs. 2.6 lakh. Bolt seals are bought through Stores and booked to the Fleet cost
centre. The cutter for bolt seals costs more than a year of plastic seals, and
each DC has two.
