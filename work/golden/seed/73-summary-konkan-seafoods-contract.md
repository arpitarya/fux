# Konkan Seafoods frozen account: per-pallet liability cap, invoice payment days, case loss allowance

The terms of the Konkan Seafoods account that the account team is asked about
most often: the most we pay for one damaged pallet, how many days the customer
has to pay an invoice, and how much unexplained case loss we absorb.

## Per-pallet liability cap for a damaged pallet

The most Quillfern pays for any one damaged pallet is Rs. 15,000. The cap was
raised from Rs. 12,000 on 1 April 2026. The yearly total is Rs. 20 lakh.

## Invoice payment days

The customer pays each invoice within 30 days of its date. Payment days were
shortened from 45 to 30 on 1 October 2025.

## Case loss allowance per quarter

Quillfern absorbs unexplained case loss of up to 0.25 per cent of cases handled
in each quarter. Loss above that is paid at the customer's invoice price.

## Notice

Either side may end the account on 90 days' written notice.
