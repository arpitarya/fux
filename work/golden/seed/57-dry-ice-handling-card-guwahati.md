# Dry ice handling card, Guwahati stores

Guwahati keeps its dry ice in the chest inside the stores cage beside Q-G2. The
chest is filled by Brahmaputra Industrial Gases on Tuesdays and Fridays.

## Before you touch it

Cryogenic gloves and the face shield from the cage rack. Never cotton gloves,
never bare hands.

## Taking it out

1. Sign the pellets out on QCL-F-26 with the kg, the job and your name.
2. Insulated tote only; never a sealed box, flask or screw-lid drum.
3. Dry ice never travels in a driver's cab and never goes into G-COLD-1.

## Frozen body top-up

4. Tote on the body floor at the rear, doors open, engine off; keep out of the body until the Guwahati night lead says it is clear.

## Afterwards

5. Unused pellets go back to the chest in the same shift.
6. In the monsoon, keep the tote covered between the cage and the dock; rain on dry ice throws up fog that hides the dock edge.
