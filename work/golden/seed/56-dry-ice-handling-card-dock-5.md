# Dry ice handling card, dock 5 board

Printed for the board beside dock 5, where frozen top-ups are loaded.

## Before you touch it

Cryogenic gloves and face shield from the rack beside the chest. No cotton
gloves, no bare hands.

## Taking it out

1. Sign the pellets out on QCL-F-26: kg, job, your name.
2. Insulated tote only. No sealed box, flask or screw-lid drum.
3. Anything above 15 kg needs two people to carry it.
4. Never in the driver's cab. Never into C2 or C3.

## Frozen body top-up

5. Tote on the body floor at the rear, doors open, engine off. Nobody goes into the body for 1 minute.
6. Empty the tote into the top-up tray, then close up.

## Afterwards

7. Unused pellets back to the chest this shift.

## If the monitor beeps

Leave, shut the door, call the DC manager.
