# Nawanshahr depot: if someone falls ill or is hurt

Pinned beside the first-aid box in the depot office at Nawanshahr.

## First thing

Shout for the nearest first aider and send someone to the gate to call the ambulance. Do not move a person who has fallen from height unless they are in danger where they lie.

## First aiders

The depot keeps one trained first aider for every 50 people on a shift. Their names and photographs are on the board beside this card, and each wears a green helmet sticker.

## The machine in the office

The depot has one defibrillator, in the marked cabinet beside the office door. Anyone may use it; it talks you through every step and will not shock unless it should.

The first aider on the morning shift checks its status light, battery and pads once a month and signs the card inside the cabinet. Pads past their date are replaced from the spare pack in the office drawer.

## Afterwards

Every incident is written in the accident book before the end of the shift, by the first aider and the shift lead together. If the aed was used, the regional safety officer is told the same day so that it can be serviced and its pads replaced.

## The hospital

The nearest hospital with a casualty department is the civil hospital, about 4 km away. Its number is on the board.

## First-aid box

The box is checked every Monday by the first aider on the morning shift, and anything used during the week is replaced from the stock in the office cupboard. An empty box is reported, not left for the next person.
