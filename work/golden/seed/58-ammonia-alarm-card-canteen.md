# Ammonia alarm card, canteen notice board

## When the site siren sounds

1. Stop forklifts and trucks, engines off. Do not drive through the plant yard.
2. Leave by the nearest door away from the compressor house.
3. Go to muster point M2 by gate 2, beside the windsock.
4. The shift lead counts heads against the gate register within 15 minutes and rings the plant operator on extension 251 with the count.
5. Nobody goes back for stock, phones or trucks.

## The compressor house

6. Plant crew only, with breathing sets.

## Going back

7. Wait at M2 until the DC manager sends the all clear on the night team group.
