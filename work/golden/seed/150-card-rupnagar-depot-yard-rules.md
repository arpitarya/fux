# Rupnagar depot: moving around the yard safely

Handed to every driver at the Rupnagar depot gate, and pinned in the drivers' rest room.

## For people on foot

Use the painted walkway along the north fence, which is 1 metre wide and marked in white. Cross the roadway only at the zebra crossing outside the office. Do not walk behind any vehicle that is reversing or about to reverse.

## For drivers

The yard speed limit is 8 km/h everywhere. Keep to the marked lanes and use your horn before reversing.

## Reversing

Every reversing manoeuvre onto a dock, by day or night, is guided by the dock marshal in the orange vest, who stands where you can see him in your mirror. If you cannot see him, stop.

## Parking

Park only in the marked bays along the south fence. Do not park on the roadway or across the gate, even for a minute.

## Visitors

Visitors walk only with an escort. Any pedestrian who has not been given a vest at the gate is stopped and taken back to the gate cabin.

## In the dark

The yard lights come on at dusk. If a light is out, tell the gate guard; nobody reverses onto a dock with a dark apron.

## Children and animals

No children are brought into the yard. Stray dogs are reported to the gate guard and are not fed.

## Breakdowns

A vehicle that breaks down in the yard puts on its hazard lights and the driver tells the gate guard, who sends the dock marshal.
