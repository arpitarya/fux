# Cold room mapping study TMS-42

Study **TMS-42** is the temperature mapping of cold room C3, the insulin room at Nagpur DC, run over 72 hours from 11 to 14 June 2024. Studies in this series are numbered one after another and the numbers differ by one digit. A study number names one room on one date, never a room in general, so quote the full number.

## 1. Scope

1.1. Empty-room run for 24 hours, then loaded-room run for 48 hours with 9 pallets of water-filled dummy cartons in the insulin positions.

1.2. Acceptance: every probe inside +2.0 C to +8.0 C for the whole run, and the room back inside limits within 15 minutes of a 3-minute door opening.

## 2. Probe grid

2.1. 18 probes at three heights (low 0.3 m, mid 1.5 m, high 2.7 m) across racks A and B, bays 1 to 3. A position is named rack, bay and height, e.g. A3-high.

2.2. Two reference loggers: one at the evaporator return, one at the door.

## 3. Results

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| hottest position | A3-high, under the roof light, max +6.9 C |
| coldest position | B2-low, in the evaporator discharge, min +2.3 C |
| MKT, whole room, loaded run | +4.6 C |
| recovery after 3-minute door opening | 9 minutes |

3.1. The Worst Case Probe (WCP) is the position that came closest to either limit during the loaded run. In C3 the WCP is B2-low, on the cold side.

## 4. Conclusion

4.1. C3 passed.

4.2. Because the WCP is a cold reading, insulin cartons are never placed on the floor-level shelf of rack B. Frozen insulin is destroyed, not released.

4.3. The permanent room probe is moved to the WCP.

## 5. Requalification

5.1. C3 is re-mapped within 24 months, or sooner after any change to the door, curtain, evaporator or racking.
