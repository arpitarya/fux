RASOI BAZAAR FROZEN PARATHA ACCOUNT

Monthly minimum pallet moves
Rasoi Bazaar commits to 1,200 pallet movements a month. Each missed movement
is charged Rs. 250.

Pallet height
Paratha pallets stand no higher than 1.6 m including the wood. Taller pallets
are restacked at the customer's cost.

Order cut-off for next-day Mumbai vans
Rasoi Bazaar orders received by 12:00 go on the next-day Mumbai vans. Later
orders go the day after.

Contacts
Account manager: Kiran Salunke. The customer's buyer calls only the account
manager, never the dock.
