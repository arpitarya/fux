# Daund sugar store laboratory: making used plates and glassware safe

For the laboratory at the Daund sugar and jaggery store, which counts yeasts and moulds in stored sugar.

## The sterilizer

Used plates and glassware are made safe in the laboratory's autoclave before they are washed or thrown away. A standard load runs at 121 C for 15 minutes, counted from the moment the load reaches temperature. The printout of every cycle is kept in the cycle file.

## Checking that it works

Once a month a biological indicator strip carrying heat-resistant spore cultures is placed in the centre of a full load. After the cycle the strip is incubated for two days. Any growth means the cycle failed: the month's loads are repeated and the machine is serviced before use.

## Loading

Bags are loosely tied so that steam can get in, and the machine is never packed so full that the door strains to close.

## Safety

Only trained staff load and unload it, wearing heat-resistant gloves and a face shield. Nobody opens the door until the pressure gauge reads zero and the temperature has fallen below 80 C.

## Faults

A fault on the display, a leak at the door seal or a cycle that does not reach temperature is reported to the laboratory supervisor, and the machine is not used until the service agent has been.

## Waste

Waste that has been through a cycle goes to the general waste contractor.
