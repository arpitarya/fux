Sahyog doorstep insulin delivery: OTP code, waiting at the door, neighbour handover

Rider sheet for the doorstep insulin drops we run for Sahyog Pharmacy in Kolhapur. Read it before your first run.

OTP code at the door

The patient reads out the six-digit OTP code from the SMS. No code means no handover.

Patient not opening the door

Wait five minutes at the door and call the patient once. If there is no answer, the pouch goes back in the carrier and you tell the hub.

Leaving insulin with a neighbour or watchman

If the patient is out, the insulin may be left with a neighbour or the building watchman, as long as that person shows photo ID and signs the delivery slip.

Photographs

Photograph the pouch at the door and upload the photo before moving off.
