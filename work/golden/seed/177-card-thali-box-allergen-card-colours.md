# Thali Box kit allergen card colours

Taped above the last station on the Kondhwa kitting line, where the allergen card goes into each kit.

## The colours

| allergen card colour | meaning |
|---|---|
| red | the kit contains peanuts or tree nuts |
| yellow | the kit contains milk or milk products but no nuts |
| blue | the kit contains gluten but no nuts or milk |
| green | the kit contains none of the allergens listed on the card |

A kit that contains more than one group gets the card for the highest group in this table: red before yellow, yellow before blue.

## Checking

The checker at the end of the line compares the card colour with the order screen before closing the lid. A wrong card means the kit is opened and the card changed, never sent as it is.

## Supply

Cards are printed by Thali Box and delivered in packs of fifty per colour.
