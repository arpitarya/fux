# Coimbatore DC site handbook

Everything that is particular to Coimbatore DC, in one place. Company-wide rules
still come from their own documents: temperature limits and excursions from
QCL-QA-SOP-17, dock booking from QCL-OPS-DOCK-03, driver hours from the Driver
Hours and Road Safety Policy. Where this handbook and a company-wide rule seem
to differ, ask the DC manager before acting, and do not assume the handbook
wins. What follows is how those rules are carried out on this site, with this
building, these people and this weather.

## 1. The site at a glance

1.1. Coimbatore DC is the smallest of the three distribution centres and the
only one south of the Deccan. It serves insulin customers across western Tamil
Nadu and the Kerala border districts, restaurant chains in Coimbatore, Tiruppur
and Erode, and a small dry-grocery account that pays for the east gate to stay
open.

1.2. The building is a single-storey shed on a raised plinth. The cold block
sits on the north side, the dry block on the south side, and the office, canteen
and lockers are in the two-storey annexe at the east end. The wash pad and the
open-sided store shed are inside the south gate, on the left as a vehicle
enters.

1.3. There is no rail siding, no mezzanine and no second floor in the cold
block. Everything moves on pallet trucks and two electric forklifts, FL-C1 and
FL-C2. FL-C2 is the only forklift rated for the cold block floor.

1.4. The site works two shifts, 06:00 to 14:00 and 14:00 to 22:00, with a
security-only night from 22:00 to 06:00. Night receipts of pharma critical stock
are not planned at Coimbatore; a vehicle that arrives at night waits outside the
south gate for the 06:00 shift unless the DC manager has approved a night
receipt in writing.

1.5. The site was opened in 2019 in a leased building. The lease is renewed in
five-year blocks, which is why some fixtures, including the canteen extractor
fan and the annexe stairs, belong to the landlord and are repaired through him.

## 2. People and phones

2.1. DC manager: S. Venkatesan. He is also the nominated escalation contact for
temperature events at Coimbatore under appendix A of the SOP.

2.2. Shift leads: Kavitha Ramasamy on the morning shift, and Arul Doss on the
afternoon shift. On the security-only night, the senior guard phones the DC
manager, never the shift leads, for anything that is not a fire.

2.3. Security is contracted to Nilgiri Guarding. The senior guard on each night
is named on the security roster pinned in the gate cabin. Guards change every
four weeks, which is why the gate cabin keeps its own copy of every card that
matters to them.

2.4. QA has no officer based at Coimbatore. QA work is done by the shift lead
with the Nagpur QA desk on the phone, extension 440 in business hours. For a
pharma critical event at any hour, the shift lead calls Compliance-Red as at
every other site.

2.5. Fleet Control is in Nagpur on extension 301. Coimbatore has no fleet desk
of its own; the shift lead is Fleet Control's eyes on the apron.

2.6. The site landline goes to the office annexe. After 22:00 it diverts to the
gate cabin, and the guard answers it with the site name only, never with a
person's name.

## 3. Getting a vehicle in and out

3.1. Reefers use the south gate. The east gate is for dry grocery only unless
the DC manager approves an emergency move, as the old wiki page also said.

3.2. School traffic on Temple Street blocks the turn into the south gate between
07:45 and 08:30. Drivers are told on booking to arrive either before 07:30 or
after 08:40. A reefer is never queued across Temple Street; if the apron is
full, the driver goes round the block and comes back.

3.3. Security holds each vehicle outside the south gate until the previous one
has cleared the apron. The apron takes one reefer and one van at a time, no
more, because a reversing reefer cannot see a second one parked behind it.

3.4. Bookings are made in Dockwise, which Coimbatore joined on 1 February 2026.
The grace table and the cancellation rule are the company rule, not a local
one, and are not repeated here.

3.5. Frozen loads are placed before lunch. After about 13:00 the afternoon sun
lies across the outer apron, drivers open their doors to check paperwork, and
the frozen body loses cold it cannot spare.

3.6. Reversing on the apron after dark needs a spotter with a torch, whatever
the vehicle's camera shows. The apron lights are on a timer that the landlord
controls, and they have twice failed to come on in the monsoon.

## 4. Rooms, bays and what lives where

4.1. CBE-PHARMA is the insulin room. It holds insulin only; Coimbatore does not
store vaccines. Room limits and excursion durations are those of the SOP and
are not repeated in this handbook.

4.2. CBE-FROZEN is the freezer. It holds restaurant-chain frozen stock and a
small amount of frozen peas for a Tiruppur wholesaler.

4.3. The chilled dairy room was closed in 2024 when the Coimbatore milk account
moved to a local cooperative. Its door is still in the corridor and is labelled
CLOSED ROOM, NO STOCK. Nothing is put in it, including overflow.

4.4. Quarantine bay Q-C1 is at the north-west corner of the cold block, behind
the red chain. Only QA, through the shift lead on the phone, releases stock from
it.

4.5. Dry grocery is stored in the south block in racks S1 to S6. None of it is
cold chain, and none of it may be stacked against the cold block wall, which
sweats in the monsoon.

4.6. The battery charging bay for FL-C1 and FL-C2 is outside the cold block,
under the annexe stairs. Nobody charges a forklift inside a cold room.

## 5. Instruments that travel south

5.1. Coimbatore has no calibration chamber and no bench master logger. Those
live at Nagpur. What Coimbatore does have is one lab-certified hand-held
thermometer, serial HP-C00, kept locked in the DC manager's cabinet.

5.2. Every hand-held thermometer in daily use at Coimbatore is checked against
HP-C00 every 14 days: both tips go into the same stirred jug of crushed ice and
water, and the two readings must agree within 0.5 C. The shift lead writes both
readings on QCL-F-12 and sends a photograph of the sheet to the Nagpur QA desk.

5.3. The shorter interval is deliberate. Coimbatore has no bench to catch a
drifting instrument quickly, and a second jug check costs nothing but ten
minutes.

5.4. HP-C00 itself goes to Metrosure Labs, Pune, by courier every 12 months.
While it is away, the Nagpur QA desk sends a loan instrument ahead of it, so the
site is never without a certified thermometer.

5.5. USB loggers are not checked at Coimbatore at all. Every spare logger for a
Coimbatore red sleeve is checked at the Nagpur bench and arrives in its sleeve
already stickered. A logger that has been out of date on the shelf is sent back
to Nagpur, never re-used on the strength of a local jug check.

5.6. Infrared guns are used at Coimbatore only to find a warm carton quickly.
They are never the reading that decides anything.

## 6. The shed by the wash pad

6.1. Coimbatore buys dry ice for two jobs only: emergency packing for a
restaurant chain whose own freezer has failed, and top-up of a frozen body that
has to wait on the apron.

6.2. The dry-ice chest stands in the open-sided store shed beside the wash pad,
not inside any building. The shed has a roof and no walls on two sides, so
carbon dioxide does not collect there the way it does in a closed store. For
that reason there is no carbon dioxide monitor at Coimbatore. The chest is never
moved indoors, even in heavy rain.

6.3. Supply and handling at Coimbatore:

| item | Coimbatore rule |
|---|---|
| supplier | Kovai Industrial Gases fills the chest with 60 kg every Tuesday |
| carrying | any tote heavier than 8 kg is carried by two people |
| gloves and shield | from the hook inside the shed |
| sign-out | QCL-F-26, kept in the shed box |

6.4. The two-person figure is lower at Coimbatore than elsewhere because the
path from the shed to the apron crosses the wash pad, which is wet for most of
the day.

6.5. Dry ice never goes into CBE-PHARMA, never into the closed dairy room, and
never into a cab. Unused pellets go back to the chest in the same shift.

## 7. Leaving the building in a hurry

7.1. The Coimbatore cold block runs on packaged refrigeration units on the roof.
There is no ammonia on this site, so the ammonia cards used at Nagpur do not
apply here and must not be pinned up here.

7.2. Coimbatore has one site siren, and it means leave the building. It sounds
for fire, for a gas smell from the canteen cylinders, and for a refrigerant leak
alarm from a roof unit.

7.3. Everyone goes to assembly point CM-1, the coconut grove corner inside the
east gate. Nobody gathers on the south apron: that is where the fire tender
comes in.

7.4. The shift lead counts against the gate register and the Dockwise day list,
and phones the count to the DC manager. Drivers waiting outside the south gate
stay outside and are counted by the guard.

7.5. Nobody goes back into the cold block for stock. Pallets left outside a room
are dealt with afterwards under the SOP.

7.6. The all clear is given in person by the DC manager or, at night, by the
senior guard after the fire service leaves.

## 8. North-east monsoon and the afternoon sun

8.1. Coimbatore's heavy rain comes with the north-east monsoon, from October to
December, not in the June to September months that set the calendar at Nagpur
and Guwahati.

8.2. Rain cover over rear pallets is used at Coimbatore from October to December
whenever it is raining on the apron. The site owns six tarpaulins for this.

8.3. Coimbatore does not bill the customer for rain cover. The monsoon handling
line on the rate card is a Guwahati charge and is not quoted for Coimbatore
loads. Customer service agents who copy the Guwahati line onto a Coimbatore
quotation create a credit note later.

8.4. The hot months are March to May. In those months the afternoon rule in
section 3.5 is enforced strictly, and the wash pad is used before 11:00 so that
washed bodies are not left drying in the midday sun with their doors open.

8.5. The cold block wall sweats in the monsoon. Anything stored against it on
the dry side is moved 30 cm away from the wall from October onwards.

## 9. Insulin without spare cold

9.1. Coimbatore holds no passive shippers, no gel packs and no conditioning
cabinet. The passive shipper pilot is at Nagpur only, and it does not extend to
Coimbatore until a later project is approved.

9.2. Because there are no shippers, insulin at Coimbatore is never staged on an
open dock beyond the SOP limit. Insulin moves from CBE-PHARMA straight into a
reefer body that is already at loading temperature, and the reefer waits for
the insulin, never the other way round.

9.3. A customer who asks Coimbatore for a shipper-packed consignment is told
that the site cannot pack one. The account manager may arrange for a
shipper-packed consignment to come from Nagpur.

9.4. Coimbatore does not stock blue-cap or white-cap packs of any kind, and does
not freeze packs in CBE-FROZEN for customers. A customer's own packs returned
with a consignment go back with the customer's return challan.

## 10. Seal books and the security cabin

10.1. Seal books for Coimbatore are issued by Stores at Nagpur and arrive by
courier two books at a time. They are kept in the locked drawer in the gate
cabin, not in the office annexe, because the gate cabin is staffed all night and
the annexe is not.

10.2. Only one seal book is open at the gate at a time. The guard hands a seal
to the shift lead; the guard never fits a seal himself.

10.3. The rest of the sealing rule is the company rule. Seal numbers go on the
dispatch note and in QCL-F-35 before the vehicle moves.

10.4. A vehicle arriving with a broken seal or a number that does not match is
kept outside the south gate, photographed, and reported to Fleet Control before
anyone opens the doors.

## 11. Canteen, lockers and shifts

11.1. The canteen opens at 05:30 and closes at 22:30. The contractor serves
breakfast, a lunch thali and evening tiffin. Drivers waiting for a slot may use
the canteen but not the lockers.

11.2. Lockers are in the annexe ground floor. Each employee has one locker and
one key. Night-shift lockers are opened with the spare key held by security,
signed out in the key register at the gate cabin.

11.3. Shift changes are at 06:00 and 14:00. The outgoing shift lead walks the
cold block with the incoming one and hands over the Dockwise day list, the
quarantine list and any open alarm.

11.4. Overtime is approved by the DC manager in advance. Staff do not stay on
past 22:00 to finish a load; the load waits for the morning shift.

11.5. The first-aid box is in the canteen, and a second box is in the gate
cabin. The nearest hospital with a casualty department is 6 km away on the
Avinashi Road.

## 12. Scrap, waste and returns

12.1. Cardboard, stretch wrap and broken pallets are separated at the back of
the south block. A scrap dealer from Kovaipudur collects cardboard and wrap
every Tuesday and pays by the kg.

12.2. Broken pallets are stacked by the east wall for the pallet repairer, who
comes on the first working day of each month.

12.3. The old chest freezer from the closed dairy room still stands by the
scrap pile. It is not used for anything and it is not to be sold until Finance
writes it off.

12.4. Customer returns are received at dock 2 and checked against the return
challan. Frozen returns go into CBE-FROZEN only after the shift lead has
checked the logger with the Nagpur QA desk on the phone.

12.5. Spoiled stock is not scrap. It follows the spoilage and destruction rules
of the SOP, and it is never given to the scrap dealer, whatever its weight.

## 13. Language and new people

13.1. Coimbatore works in Tamil. Training is given by a trainer in Tamil, with
English slides available but never used instead of a trainer.

13.2. New joiners spend their first week on the morning shift only. They are not
put on the late shift, and never left alone in the cold block, until a shift
lead has signed them off.

13.3. Every new joiner is shown the gate cabin, the security roster and the key
register on the first day. They are told that security will not open anything
for them at night except in an emergency, and that the lockers, the cabinet and
the seal drawer are all separate keys.

13.4. New drivers from hired-truck vendors are shown the south gate routine by
the guard and the Temple Street timing by the shift lead before their first
booking.

13.5. The words used on site follow the company glossary. A new joiner who hears
a word they do not know is expected to ask, on any shift, and nobody at
Coimbatore is to laugh at the question.

## 14. Housekeeping and pests

14.1. The cold block floor is swept at the end of each shift and mopped once a
day, after the last frozen load of the morning. Mopping is never done while a
pallet is moving, because a wet floor in front of FL-C2 is how a pallet ends up
on its side.

14.2. Cardboard dust is the main dirt in the dry block. The racks S1 to S6 are
dusted on the first Saturday of the month, top shelf first, and the floor is
swept after the dusting, not before.

14.3. The pest control contractor, Kongu Hygiene Services, visits on the second
Wednesday of every month and on call after any sighting. Bait stations are
numbered on the wall plan in the office annexe. Nobody moves a bait station to
make room for stock; the contractor moves it and redraws the plan.

14.4. Birds nest in the open-sided shed and under the apron canopy. The netting
over the canopy is checked after every storm. A bird inside the cold block is
reported to the shift lead at once, and the room door is kept shut until the
contractor has taken it out.

14.5. The canteen is cleaned by the canteen contractor, not by site staff. Any
complaint about the canteen goes to the DC manager in writing.

## 15. Visitors and audits

15.1. Every visitor signs the visitor book at the gate cabin and wears a visitor
badge for the whole visit. A visitor who has not signed in is not shown round,
whoever they say they are.

15.2. Customer auditors are escorted by the DC manager or a shift lead from the
gate to the gate. They may look at any room and any record they ask for, but
they do not handle stock and they do not enter a cold room without a freezer
jacket from the rack by the corridor.

15.3. Photographs inside the cold block are taken only with the DC manager's
permission, and only of the thing being audited. A customer's stock is never
photographed for another customer.

15.4. Auditors from the landlord's insurer visit once a year. They look at the
building, the fire equipment and the roof units, not at the cold chain. The DC
manager keeps the fire extinguisher service tags and the roof unit service
sheets in one folder for them.

15.5. After any audit the DC manager writes a short note of what was asked,
what was shown and what was promised, and sends it to Revathi Iyer and Farhan
Qureshi the same week. Promises made to an auditor are kept in that note, not
in anyone's memory.

15.6. School and college groups are not shown round the cold block. Requests
for such visits are passed to Pune HQ.
