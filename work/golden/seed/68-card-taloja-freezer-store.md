# Taloja freezer store card: blast cell, order cut-off, gas leak siren

Pinned at the Taloja order desk. This card covers three things: the arrival
probe and the blast cell, the order cut-off for next-day vans, and where to
line up when the gas leak siren sounds.

## Arrival probe and blast cell

Probe one carton from the middle of each incoming pallet, with the probe in the
product core. A pallet whose core is warmer than -15 C on the arrival probe
goes to the blast cell first. The target is a core of -18 C within eight hours
of the cell door closing. A pallet still short of target at hour eight is held
and QA is told.

## Order cut-off for next-day vans

Orders received by 16:00 go on the next day's vans, and orders received after
16:00 go the day after. The cut-off moved from 17:00 on 1 September 2025, when
the first van wave moved to 05:30.

## Gas leak siren

1. Stop forklifts where they stand and switch off the engines.
2. Line up at the main gate car park. Do not go back for stock or phones.
3. Wait for the plant lead's all clear in person.
