# Quillfern Cold Chain Glossary

Plain meanings for expressions used on site. Short forms are defined in the document that uses them, never here, so an expression below appears in its long form only.

back-to-cold — the moment a staged pallet crosses back over the cold-room threshold; staging time stops here.
chlorine contact time — the minutes a sanitising solution must stay wet on a reefer floor before rinsing; wiping it dry early starts the count again.
dead zone — a stretch of road where the telematics unit cannot report; the USB logger covers it and the gap is recorded as missing, never filled in.
door open minutes — the total minutes a cold-room or reefer door stood open in one shift, summed from the door sensor; a measure of load, not a fault.
holding lane B — the covered waiting lane at Nagpur for pharma and frozen vehicles that miss their dock time; nothing is unloaded there.
mean kinetic temperature — the single steady temperature that would give stock the same total heat stress as the uneven record it actually saw; stability letters are written against it, not against the average.
pre-cool hold time — the minutes an empty reefer body is held at loading temperature, doors shut, before the first pallet goes in.
probe grid — the layout of loggers used when a room is mapped; each position is named by rack, bay and height, such as A1-high.
relief point — a named fuel plaza or rest area on a qualified route where a relief driver or a mobile service van can meet the vehicle.
seal watch — time a driver spends guarding a sealed vehicle; it is duty time and never counts as rest.
shipper conditioning time — the hours a passive shipper's gel packs must sit at their set temperature before the shipper may leave with stock.
stability budget remaining — the minutes above the high limit that a customer's stability letter still allows for one lot, once every earlier excursion on that lot is subtracted.
warm pallet — night-shift name for a pallet whose logger has alarmed but whose excursion QA has not yet confirmed; it is handled as quarantine stock until QA decides.
white tag — the RELEASED tag fixed beside a red hold tag when QA lets stock out of quarantine; the red tag stays on.
