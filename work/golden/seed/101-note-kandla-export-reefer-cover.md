# Kandla export reefer cover: notice to insurer, deductible, reefer breakdown hours, surveyor threshold, salvage of damaged pharma, settlement days

The cover on export reefer containers moved between Gandhidham and Kandla port
under policy EXR-26. This note covers notice to the insurer, the deductible,
reefer breakdown hours, the surveyor threshold, salvage of damaged pharma and
settlement days.

## Notice to insurer

Written notice goes to the insurer within 3 days of discovering a loss.

## Deductible

The deductible is Rs. 15,000 per container.

## Reefer breakdown hours

Spoilage after a reefer breakdown is covered when the unit was stopped for
12 consecutive hours or more.

## Surveyor threshold

The insurer appoints a surveyor for any loss estimated above Rs. 1 lakh.

## Salvage of damaged pharma

Damaged pharma cartons may be sold as salvage, through a buyer the insurer
approves, once the surveyor has released them.

## Settlement days

The insurer pays an agreed claim within 15 days of the survey report.
