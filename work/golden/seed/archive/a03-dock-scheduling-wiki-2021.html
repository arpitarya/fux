Dock scheduling rules (2021)

Retired page. Kept for audit history. Do not book from this page.

Nagpur hub

Book Nagpur slots by email to the dock desk before 18:00 on the previous day. There is no Dockwise calendar yet; the dock desk keeps a paper board.

Pharma uses dock 2, dairy uses docks 3 and 4, and frozen food uses dock 1. The dock numbering was changed in 2023 and this page was never updated.

No unloading between 13:30 and 14:15 for the ammonia plant defrost check.

Guwahati and Coimbatore

Both sites assign slots by phone. Guwahati calls the yard supervisor; Coimbatore calls the security desk. Neither site has a calendar.
