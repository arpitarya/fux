# Reefer asset file RF-117 (retired)

**RF-117** was an owned chilled reefer based at Nagpur DC from April 2016 until
it was retired on 10 September 2024. This file is kept for audit history only.
**Do not book RF-117.** The asset number still appears on 2016 to 2024 dispatch
notes, customer claim files and Kalpa trip exports, which is the only reason the
file survives.

RF-117 was replaced on the Nagpur chilled roster by
[RF-118](../16-reefer-RF-118-asset-file.md). They are different vehicles. A
claim, a challan or an audit query naming RF-117 concerns a vehicle that has not
moved Quillfern stock since 2024.

## Identity as retired

| field | value |
|---|---|
| asset id | RF-117 |
| chassis year | 2016 |
| body | 14-pallet chilled, dual compartment |
| base | Nagpur DC |
| Kalpa device | KFS-2014 generation, trip prefix KLP |
| Tessaline sensor | never fitted |
| retired | 10 September 2024 |

## Why it was retired

The evaporator coil corroded after the 2023 and 2024 monsoons and the body
seals no longer held a stable chilled temperature during long door-open
cross-dock work. A replacement coil was quoted at more than half the residual
value of the vehicle. Fleet recommended retirement rather than repair and
Finance agreed on 2 September 2024.

RF-117 was sold to a dry-goods operator on 28 November 2024 with the reefer unit
removed. The Kalpa device was returned to the vendor.

## What still refers to RF-117

- Kalpa trip exports for 2016 to 2024, which carry the KLP prefix. The routing
  and vendor codes of that era are described in
  [the Kalpa Alert Routing Guide](a02-kalpa-alert-routing-guide-2021.md).
- Two open customer claim files from the 2024 dairy season.
- The 2019 driver hours acknowledgements signed by its regular drivers, under
  [the 2019 Driver Hours and Road Discipline Policy](a04-driver-hours-policy-2019.md).

None of these make RF-117 a bookable asset. For anything operational, read the
live file for [RF-118](../16-reefer-RF-118-asset-file.md).
