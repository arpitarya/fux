# Driver Hours and Road Discipline Policy (2019)

Issued to all Quillfern drivers and hired-truck providers. Kept for record.

## 1. Daily limits

1.1. No driver shall be planned for more than 10 hours on duty in a duty day.
The driving component shall not exceed 8 hours.

1.2. No driver shall drive more than 5 hours continuously without a break of at
least 20 minutes.

1.3. A minimum rest period of 9 consecutive hours is required between duty days.

1.4. Weekly rest is one full day in each calendar week. Fleet Control plans it
with the depot roster.

## 2. Speed

2.1. The maximum permitted speed for a loaded Quillfern reefer is 65 km/h on
open highway, 55 km/h in rain or fog, and 40 km/h on ghat roads.

## 3. Exceptions

3.1. Where weather or police action would cause a driver to exceed a limit, the
driver reports to Fleet Control by phone and Fleet Control decides. There is no
exception code and no written exception form in this policy.

3.2. A cold-chain emergency is a valid reason to extend driving hours with Fleet
head approval. This clause was removed in later policy and must not be applied.

## 4. Conduct

4.1. Mobile phone use while driving is discouraged.

4.2. Seat belts are mandatory for the driver.

4.3. Alcohol is prohibited during duty and for four hours before duty.

## 5. Records

5.1. Dispatch notes and gate registers are kept for two years.
