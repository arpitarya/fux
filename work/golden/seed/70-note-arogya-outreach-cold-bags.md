# Arogya outreach cold bags: carrier turn-back hour, last evening run, undelivered insulin pouches

For riders on the Arogya Outreach Trust insulin drops in Solapur. This note
covers when a carrier heads back to the hub, the last evening run, and what
happens to undelivered insulin pouches.

## Carrier turn-back hour

A cold-bag carrier on the road heads back to the hub at the end of hour 3,
counted from the moment the lid is shut. Until the CB-3 bags were replaced in
November 2025, the turn-back was at the end of hour 2.

## Last evening run

No rider leaves the hub on an evening run after 20:00. Orders still waiting at
20:00 are moved to the next morning.

## Undelivered insulin pouches

Undelivered pouches come back to the hub fridge. Once the hub lead has checked
each pouch's freeze indicator, the pouches go back on the shelf for the next
day's drops.

## Rain

Every carrier wears its rain cover from June to September.
