# Ambad workshop maintenance apprentices: programme length, intake size, stipend, mentor load, tool kit at completion, file retention

For the vehicle maintenance apprentices at the Ambad workshop in Nashik. This
card covers programme length, intake size, stipend, mentor load, the tool kit
at completion and how long apprentice files are kept.

## Programme length

The apprenticeship lasts 30 months.

## Intake size

One intake each April, of 20 apprentices.

## Stipend

Every apprentice receives a stipend of Rs. 11,500 a month throughout.

## Mentor load

A mentor may take up to four apprentices at a time.

## Tool kit at completion

The tool kit stays workshop property and is handed back at completion.

## File retention

Apprentice files are kept for 3 years after the apprentice leaves.
