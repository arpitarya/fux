# Kulfi Corner insulated crates: getting them back from the shops

For the small insulated crates in which we deliver Kulfi Corner's kulfi to paan shops and sweet shops that have no freezer of their own.

## Why crates go missing

A shop keeps the kulfi in the crate until it sells out, and an empty crate makes a good stool. Without a reason to hand it back, it stays.

## The scheme

Each shop pays a deposit of Rs. 30 per crate with its first order. The money is refunded in full against any crate handed back to our driver within 7 days of the delivery it came on. A crate kept longer is treated as sold to the shop and the money is kept by Kulfi Corner.

## Collecting

Drivers collect empty crates on every visit and write the count on the delivery slip, signed by the shopkeeper.

## Broken crates

A crate that comes back cracked is still counted and refunded; the shop is not charged for wear. Broken crates go back to the maker, which grinds them and moulds new ones, since the crate body is recyclable plastic.

## Counting

The depot counts crates out and back every week and sends the figures to Kulfi Corner's accounts team.

## Questions

Shopkeepers' questions about refunds go to Kulfi Corner's own helpline, printed on the crate lid.
