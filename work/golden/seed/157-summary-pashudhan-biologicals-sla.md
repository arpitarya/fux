# Pashudhan Biologicals account: the delivery promise and what we owe when we miss it

Summary for the account team of the service terms with Pashudhan Biologicals, which makes poultry vaccines and ships them from its Hadapsar plant to hatcheries and feed dealers across Maharashtra and Karnataka.

## The promise

An order counts as met only if it arrives on the agreed day, inside the hatchery's receiving window, complete and undamaged. Pashudhan measures us on the share of orders met each month.

## The figure

The monthly target is 95 per cent. For each full percentage point below it, Pashudhan gets a credit of 1 per cent of that month's transport invoice, up to a maximum of 5 per cent. Credits come off the next invoice.

## Exclusions

An order missed because a hatchery was closed, because Pashudhan changed it after dispatch, or because a state border was shut is left out of the otif count, provided we told Pashudhan the cause within two working days.

## Two bad months

A shortfall in two months running is raised by Pashudhan at the quarterly review, and we bring a written plan to fix it.

## Reports

The account manager sends the month's figures, with the order list behind them, by the 5th working day of the next month.

## Contacts

Our account manager and Pashudhan's logistics manager meet monthly. The contract file holds the full terms.
