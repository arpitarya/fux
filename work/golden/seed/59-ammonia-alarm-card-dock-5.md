# Ammonia alarm card, dock 5

Dock 5 is the frozen dock and the nearest dock to the compressor house, so this
card hangs at the shutter.

## When the site siren sounds

1. Stop the forklift where it is and switch it off. If a reefer is backing in, the driver stops and switches off.
2. Leave through the dock 5 side door, away from the compressor house.
3. Go to muster point M2 by gate 2, beside the windsock.
4. Give your name to the shift lead at M2.
5. Nobody goes back for stock, a phone or a truck.

## The compressor house

6. Only the plant crew go in.

## Going back

7. Stay at M2 until the shift lead tells you the dock is open again.
