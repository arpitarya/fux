# Hosur depot cage: who has to OK a buy, pellet deliveries in kg and days

Hangs at the Hosur cage counter.

## Who has to OK a buy for the cage

| amount | who OKs it |
|---|---|
| up to Rs. 50,000 | depot lead |
| above Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 3 lakh | regional finance |
| above Rs. 3 lakh | Managing Director |

## Pellet deliveries

The pellet chest gets 80 kg per delivery, on Wednesday and Saturday mornings.
