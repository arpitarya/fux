# Dera Bassi pharma store: who signs the rooms over

For the two chilled rooms being built inside the Dera Bassi pharma store, which will hold one customer's biologics under a dedicated contract.

## Why the customer signs

The customer paid for the rooms and its regulator inspects them, so its contract asks for a bigger part in accepting them than our usual customers take.

## The tests

Every test in the plan, from the pressure and tightness tests to the temperature runs, is watched by three signatories: our site QA lead, the refrigeration contractor's engineer and the customer's validation manager. None may sign a test they did not watch.

## The record

Each test sheet carries the three signatures and the date. A test that was not witnessed by all three is repeated, however inconvenient for the contractor.

## Mapping

Temperature mapping follows handover and is run by our QA team with the customer's validation manager present.

## If someone cannot come

The customer may name a deputy in writing, before the test, to sign in its validation manager's place. We may not; our QA lead's deputy must be named on the plan.

## Copies

Copies of every signed sheet go to the customer and to our QA office on the day of the test. The full record is bound and handed over in two copies when the last test is signed, one for each company.

## Delays

If the customer's validation manager cannot attend a planned test, the test is moved rather than run without them. The contractor is told at least a day ahead.
