# Refrigeration apprenticeship: outline for the 2025 intake

## Length and intake

The programme lasts 24 months. One intake of 10 apprentices joins each January.

## Stipend

Apprentices are paid a flat Rs. 12,000 a month for the whole programme.

## Gas handling certificate

The gas handling certificate is sat at the end of month six.

## Marks and attendance

Practical tasks pass at 65 per cent. Attendance must be at least 80 per cent.

## On live units

One qualified technician supervises up to four apprentices on live equipment.
Apprentices may join night work from month six.

## Mentors

A mentor takes up to three apprentices.
