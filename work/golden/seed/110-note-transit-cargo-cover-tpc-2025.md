# Transit cargo cover TPC 2025-26: deductible, notice to insurer, machinery stoppage, per-vehicle limit, surveyor

Summary of our Transit Protection Cover for the policy year 1 April 2025 to
31 March 2026, for site managers and planners.

## Deductible

Each consignment bears a deductible of Rs. 25,000.

## Notice to insurer

Written notice reaches the insurer within 14 days of discovery.

## Machinery stoppage

Spoilage after a refrigeration stoppage is covered when the unit stopped for
8 consecutive hours or more.

## Per-vehicle limit

The insurer pays at most Rs. 80 lakh for one vehicle on one trip.

## Surveyor

A surveyor is appointed for losses estimated above Rs. 2 lakh.

## Papers and payment

Claim papers go in within 60 days. The insurer pays within 45 days of the
survey report. Strikes and riots are not covered.
