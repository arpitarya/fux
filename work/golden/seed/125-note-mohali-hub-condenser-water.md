# Mohali hub: water for the condensers

For the plant operators at the Mohali hub in the industrial area, phase 8.

## Where the water comes from

Mohali has no reliable municipal supply for industrial use, so the evaporative condensers on the plant room roof take their water from the borewell behind the generator plinth. Drinking water is bought in by tanker and never mixed with it.

## Softening

All plant water passes through the duplex softener before it reaches the roof. The softener regenerates automatically on a meter, and the salt tank is topped up every Monday.

## Testing

The plant operator tests the softened water every day at 10:00 with the hand-held meter. The tds reading must stay below 300 ppm, because the Mohali water is harder than most and the condensers scale quickly; above that figure, the bleed valve is opened one turn and the softener is regenerated by hand.

Every reading goes on the sheet pinned in the plant room, with the time and the operator's initials. The meter is checked against its standard solution every Monday.

## Cleaning and dosing

The condenser sumps are drained and cleaned every two months. The water-treatment contractor visits monthly and doses biocide under the chart kept in the plant room folder.

## When the supply fails

If the pump fails or the softener alarms, the operator phones the regional engineer at once. The condensers can run for a few hours on the water in their sumps, but not through an afternoon in May.
