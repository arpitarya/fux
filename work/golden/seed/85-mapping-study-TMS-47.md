# Cold room mapping study TMS-47

Study **TMS-47** is the temperature mapping of cold room D1, the milk room at Nagpur DC, run over 72 hours from 15 to 18 June 2026. D1 holds crates stacked in floor lanes, not racking, so its grid is laid out by lane.

## 1. Scope

1.1. Empty-room run for 24 hours, then loaded-room run for 48 hours with 420 crates of water-filled milk pouches stacked in the seven floor lanes. The loaded run included two morning dispatch peaks, with the door opened on an ordinary weekday's dispatch timetable.

1.2. Acceptance: every probe inside the dairy storage range in clause 2.3 of QCL-QA-SOP-17 for the whole run, and the room back inside range within 18 minutes of a 2-minute door opening.

## 2. Probe grid

2.1. 21 probes at three heights (low 0.2 m, mid 1.0 m, high 1.8 m, the top of a full crate stack) across floor lanes L1 to L7, one column per lane at mid-length. A position is named by lane and height, e.g. L4-mid.

2.2. Two reference loggers: one at the evaporator return, one at the strip curtain.

## 3. Results

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| warmest position | L1-high, nearest the door, max +3.8 C |
| coldest position | L6-low, under the evaporator, min +0.6 C |
| MKT, whole room, loaded run | +2.4 C |
| recovery after 2-minute door opening | 13 minutes |

3.1. The WCP is L1-high, on the warm side. Both its peaks came during the morning dispatch, not during the timed door test.

## 4. Conclusion

4.1. D1 passed.

4.2. During May and June the top layer of lane L1 carries only empty crates.

4.3. The permanent room probe moves to L1-high.

4.4. No lane is stacked above 1.8 m. This study does not cover anything higher.

## 5. Requalification

5.1. D1 is re-mapped within 18 months, or sooner after any change to the door, the evaporator or the lane layout.
