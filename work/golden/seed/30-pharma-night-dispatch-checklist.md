# Pharma Critical Night Dispatch Checklist

Worked through at the gate by the Fleet controller and the shift lead together before any pharma critical vehicle leaves a Quillfern DC between 22:00 and 05:00. Both sign. One signature is not a dispatch.

## Before loading

1. Two drivers are named on the dispatch note before departure, as clause 2.3 of the [Driver Hours and Road Safety Policy](08-driver-hours-and-safety-policy.md) requires.

2. The body is run empty with its doors shut until the display holds loading temperature, and then held there for the Pre-Cool Hold Time (PCH) set by the qualification record for that route. Where no qualification record exists, the PCH is 45 minutes.

3. The red sleeve is in the cab locker and holds a spare calibrated logger. No logger, no departure: a hand-held thermometer is not a substitute.

4. Where the qualification record requires a backup passive shipper, its SCT is written in the shipper register. A shipper with no SCT entry is not a backup and the run waits.

## At the gate

5. A test alarm is sent to Compliance-Red from the vehicle's Tessaline unit, and the QA night phone confirms receipt before the seal goes on.

6. If the Tessaline unit has not reported for 5 consecutive minutes, the vehicle does not leave.

7. Diesel is at least three-quarters of a tank, and the reefer unit's own fuel tank is full.

8. The seal is photographed with the dock clock in frame.

## Signing

9. Controller and shift lead sign. The signed copy goes in the Fleet blue file and is kept for seven years.

10. Nothing on this checklist is skipped for a customer-declared emergency. A late vaccine is a customer problem; a vaccine that left without these checks is a Quillfern one.

## Revision note

Revision 2.0 (10 November 2025) added item 5 after the Tessaline cutover; revision 1.0 of March 2025 used a phone call to the DC manager instead.
