# Ludhiana DC utilities note (commissioning issue)

Written while Ludhiana DC was being commissioned, so that the first shift leads and the electrical supervisor had something to work to before a full manual existed.

## Substation

The transformer oil is sampled by the testing laboratory for its dielectric strength. A sample is taken once a year, and the breakdown voltage must be at least 40 kV; below that, the oil is filtered within fifteen days.

## Power factor

The capacitor bank is sized in kvar for the first year's load: 240 in four steps. Target monthly average 0.95.

## Comms room

The ups in the comms room carries the controls, the alarm panel and the servers; its autonomy is set below. On battery it must run for 20 minutes at full load. Batteries are replaced every five years.

## Roof plant

Panels are washed once a month. Each inverter is looked at by the solar contractor once a quarter. Losses from soiling are not yet measured.

## Plant water

Condenser water comes from the borewell through the softener, and its tds is checked. Softened water must stay below 800 ppm, tested monthly.

## Utility orders

The sanctioned load is 1,200 kW. On a curtailment order, switch off first the ambient store ventilation, then the yard lights, then the office air conditioning.

## Generators

Two sets, automatic changeover, tested monthly. Bulk tank dipped weekly.
