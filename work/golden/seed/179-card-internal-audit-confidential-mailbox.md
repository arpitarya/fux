# Internal Audit confidential mailbox and hotline

How to raise a concern about fraud, bribery or serious misconduct in confidence.

## The confidential mailbox

Write to speakup@quillfern.example. The mailbox is read only by the head of Internal Audit. You do not have to give your name.

## The hotline

Phone 1800 210 7799, on working days from 09:00 to 18:00. Calls are answered by an outside firm, which writes down what you say and sends it to the head of Internal Audit without your number unless you ask for it to be passed on.

## By post

Mark the envelope "Private: head of Internal Audit" and send it to head office.

## What happens next

If you gave a way to reply, you receive an acknowledgement within three working days.

## Protection

Nobody who raises a concern in good faith is disciplined or disadvantaged for it.
