# Uruli dairy plant lab: checking the measuring tools

On the wall above the balance in the laboratory we run at the dairy plant at Uruli Kanchan.

## Why

Milk tests depend on measuring small volumes of milk and reagent exactly. A tool that delivers too much or too little gives a wrong fat or a wrong count, and the farmers are paid on those numbers.

## The automatic measuring tools

Every pipette in daily use is checked every six months by weighing the water it delivers, ten times at its full volume. The average must be within 2 per cent of the volume marked on it.

## The balance

The balance is checked every morning with the certified 100 g weight. A reading more than 0.01 g out stops the gravimetric work for the day until the balance has been serviced.

## Glass measures

Glass measuring cylinders and burettes are bought with a maker's certificate and are not checked again unless they are chipped. A chipped piece is thrown away.

## Records

Every check goes on the sheet in the green file, with the tool's number, the date and the tester's initials.

## Dropped tools

Any measuring tool that has been dropped is checked before use, whatever it looks like.

## Repairs

Faulty tools go back to the maker's agent in Pune. The lab keeps two spares of each size so that testing never stops while a tool is away.
