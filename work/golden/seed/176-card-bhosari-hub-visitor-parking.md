# Bhosari hub visitor parking and gate passes

For visitors and for the security guards at the Bhosari hub gate.

## Visitor parking

Visitor parking is in bays V1 to V8, outside the gate on the estate road, opposite the tea stall of the neighbouring unit. Visitors do not park inside the yard, which is kept for trucks and vans.

Two-wheelers park in the covered stand to the left of the gate.

## Gate passes

Every visitor shows photo identity at the gate cabin and is given a printed gate pass with their name, the person they are visiting and the time in. The gate pass is valid only for that day and is handed back at the gate on leaving.

## Escort

The person being visited collects the visitor from the gate cabin and walks them back at the end.

## After hours

The hub does not receive visitors on Sundays or after 23:00.
