# Kesar Sweets festival gift boxes: the outer shipping carton

Specification for the outer carton used when we deliver Kesar Sweets' chilled mithai gift boxes to corporate customers in the Diwali and Rakhi seasons.

## What it carries

Each outer carries six gift boxes of mithai, each in its own printed tin or card box, with two cold packs between the layers. Outers travel in chilled vans and are stacked up to eight high in the van and in the customer's office store room.

## Board and style

The outer is a seven-ply corrugated regular slotted carton, plain brown with Kesar Sweets' logo printed in one colour on two sides. Seven-ply was chosen because corporate store rooms stack cartons higher than any doorstep does.

## Strength

The carton's strength in the bct must be at least 600 kgf, measured by the box maker on conditioned samples. The box maker sends the certificate with every lot, and our receiving clerk files it.

## Closure

Cartons are closed with brown paper tape, never plastic, across the top and down both ends.

## Labels

One address label on the top, one festival handling label on a side. Nothing is written by hand on the carton.

## Storage

Empty cartons are stored flat, off the floor, away from damp walls, for no more than three months before use.

## Approval

Changes to this specification need Kesar Sweets' written agreement and ours.
