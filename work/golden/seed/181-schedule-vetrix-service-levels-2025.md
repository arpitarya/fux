# Service Level Schedule, Contract VX-09 (first contract year)

Revision 1, for the first contract year from 1 April 2025. Replaced at renewal.

## Delivery promise

Orders must arrive complete, undamaged and inside the delivery window. The otif target is a monthly figure. At least 95 per cent of orders each month must meet it. For each full point of shortfall below that, Vetrix receives a credit of 2 per cent of the month's handling invoice.

## Disease alerts

When a state notifies an outbreak, Vetrix may mark orders as urgent. Each is dispatched within 12 hours of receipt. Quillfern and Vetrix agree a daily ceiling if a surge exceeds what can be delivered safely.

## Monthly report

Each kpi is defined in this schedule. The report and its one-page scorecard reach Vetrix by the 10th working day of the following month.

## Frozen breeding stock

Straws are held in storage dewar units at Ludhiana DC. Each unit is topped up every 14 days. The level must not fall below 40 per cent of capacity.

## Liability

The indemnity for one consignment is limited to Rs. 5 lakh. The aggregate in a contract year is limited. The yearly figure is Rs. 40 lakh.

## Meetings

Monthly operations review. A steering group of senior managers is set up. It meets once a year, in October. Changes to this schedule are made only through these governance meetings.
