# Consolidated Service Terms, Contract CN-31

The terms on which Quillfern Cold Logistics Pvt. Ltd. stores and delivers frozen
products for Saptagiri Foods Pvt. Ltd., as they stand after the two amendments.
This consolidation is prepared by Finance for the people who run the account. It
replaces nothing; the signed agreement and its amendments remain the legal
record, and where this text and the signed record differ, the signed record
applies.

## 1. Parties and how to read this

1.1. The parties are Quillfern Cold Logistics Pvt. Ltd. ("Quillfern") and
Saptagiri Foods Pvt. Ltd. ("the customer"), a maker of frozen parathas, samosas
and snacks with a factory at Chakan.

1.2. The agreement began on 1 April 2025 and runs until either side ends it under section 10.

1.3. Amendment 1 took effect on 1 October 2025 and changed the payment terms.
Amendment 2 took effect on 1 April 2026 and changed the liability cap. Both are
folded into the text below and listed in section 15.

1.4. Clause numbers here follow this consolidation, not the original agreement.
A clause quoted from the signed record carries its original number.

## 2. The work

2.1. Quillfern collects the customer's frozen products from the Chakan factory,
stores them and delivers them to restaurants, caterers and retailers in Pune
and Mumbai.

2.2. Pune deliveries go out from Quillfern's own stock. Mumbai-bound stock is
cross-docked at Quillfern's Bhiwandi site and goes out on the next morning's
vans.

2.3. Call-off times for Mumbai deliveries are those printed in the site handbook of the cross-dock that ships them.

2.4. Waiting charges on the customer's own vehicles at the cross-dock are billed at the site's published rate less 20 per cent.

2.5. Pallets are exchanged one for one at the cross-dock. Exchange units that arrive unfit on the customer's own vehicles are charged at the site figure, with no discount.

2.6. Quillfern does not sell, promote or price the customer's products and does
not take orders from the customer's buyers directly.

## 3. Commitments on both sides

3.1. The customer commits to 1,800 pallet movements in each calendar month; any month that falls short is charged Rs. 300 for each movement not made.

3.2. A pallet movement is one pallet received or one pallet dispatched. A pallet
received and dispatched in the same month counts twice.

3.3. Quillfern commits to hold enough frozen space in Pune for 900 of the
customer's pallets at any one time, and to give 30 days' notice before reducing
it.

3.4. Months in which Quillfern could not take the customer's stock, for reasons
on Quillfern's side, are not counted for the shortfall charge.

## 4. Product, packing and handover

4.1. The customer packs its products in cartons that carry the batch number and
the best-before date on two faces.

4.2. Loaded pallets stand no higher than 1.8 metres including the wooden base. Taller pallets are restacked at the customer's cost before they are
accepted.

4.3. Each pallet is stretch-wrapped and labelled with the customer's pallet ID
on two faces.

4.4. Handover takes place when the pallet crosses the dock edge at the
receiving site. The receiving shift lead's count is the count.

## 5. Temperatures

5.1. The customer's products are kept at -18 C or colder in storage and in
transit.

5.2. The customer pre-cools each trailer before loading at Chakan and sends the
pre-cool record with the trailer.

5.3. Product that turns up above minus 12 is brought down to temperature at the cross-dock, and that work is invoiced to the customer.

5.4. Quillfern is not liable for product damage caused by temperatures before
handover. Each party may inspect the other's temperature records for its
stock.

## 6. Counting

6.1. Stock is counted in cases at receipt and at dispatch, and a full count of
the customer's stock is taken at the end of each quarter.

6.2. Quillfern carries no liability for unexplained case shortages up to 0.15 per cent of cases handled in each quarter. Shortages above that figure are paid
at the customer's invoice price to its distributors, less 10 per cent.

6.3. A shortage that is explained, such as a carton damaged in handling and
recorded at the time, is dealt with under section 7, not here.

6.4. The customer may attend the quarterly count with two people of its own.

## 7. When things go wrong

7.1. Quillfern is liable for loss of or damage to the customer's products while
in its care, subject to the limits below and to section 5.4.

7.2. For any single pallet the ceiling is Rs. 25,000, and the total for a contract year is Rs. 40 lakh.

7.3. Amendment 2 raised it from Rs. 18,000 with effect from 1 April 2026. The
contract-year total was not changed.

7.4. Neither party is liable to the other for lost profit or for loss of
business.

7.5. A claim is made in writing within 30 days of the event, with photographs
and the count records.

## 8. Price movement

8.1. Quillfern's charges are those in the schedule of rates signed with the
agreement, as moved under this section.

8.2. Rates move once a year on 1 April, by no more than the change in the wholesale price index over the previous twelve months plus two points.

8.3. Quillfern sends the new rates by 1 March. The customer may ask for the
working behind them and Quillfern will show it.

8.4. Fuel is not part of the rates. A fuel surcharge applies to transport on
Quillfern's standard terms in force on the day of travel.

## 9. Paperwork after the month

9.1. Quillfern invoices monthly, within five working days of the month's end,
with one invoice for storage and one for transport.

9.2. Since Amendment 1 of 1 October 2025 the customer settles each invoice within 45 days of its date; the original agreement allowed 60.

9.3. Late payment carries interest at 1.25 per cent a month on the overdue
amount.

9.4. Disputed lines are raised within 15 days of the invoice. The undisputed
part of an invoice is paid on time whatever the dispute.

## 10. Ending the agreement

10.1. Either side may bring it to an end by giving 120 days' written notice.

10.2. Either side may end it at once if the other fails to pay, or fails to
perform a material obligation, and does not put it right within 30 days of a
written demand.

10.3. On ending, Quillfern returns the customer's stock to Chakan or to a place
the customer names in Pune, at the customer's cost, within 15 days.

## 11. Disputes

11.1. The parties first try to settle a dispute between their account managers,
then between their directors.

11.2. A dispute not settled within 45 days goes to a sole arbitrator in Pune.

## 12. Insurance

12.1. Quillfern insures the customer's stock in its care up to the contract-year
total in section 7.2.

12.2. The customer insures its stock before handover and after delivery.

## 13. Audit and records

13.1. The customer may audit the records about its own stock twice a year on
ten working days' notice.

13.2. Records are kept for three years after the end of the agreement.

## 14. Contacts and notices

14.1. Quillfern's account manager for this contract is Neha Bhosale in Finance.
The customer's contact is its supply chain head.

14.2. Notices under this agreement are sent by registered post and by e-mail to
the addresses in the signed record.
