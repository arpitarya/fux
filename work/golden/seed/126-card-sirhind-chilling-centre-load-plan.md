# Sirhind milk chilling centre: what to switch off when the utility calls

Pinned in the plant room of the chilling centre we run for the dairy cooperative at Sirhind.

## Who takes the call

The utility phones the centre manager, or the shift lead at night, with the hours and the load limit. The shift lead writes both on this card's clipboard and starts the list below at once.

## The list

The centre's sanctioned load is 400 kW. During a curtailment order, switch off in this order until the meter shows the centre under the figure the utility gave:

1. The staff quarters' water heaters.
2. The office air conditioning.
3. The yard lights, except the gate light and the tanker bay light.
4. The crate washer.

The milk chillers, the ice-bank compressors and the reception pumps are never switched off, whatever the order says. If the list does not bring the centre under the figure, start the generator and take the chillers onto it.

## Afterwards

When the order ends, switch everything back on in the reverse order, one item every five minutes, so that the meter does not record a peak. Write the times on the clipboard sheet and leave it for the centre manager.

## Telling the cooperative

The centre manager tells the cooperative's milk procurement office if the order runs into the evening collection, so that tankers can be held at the villages rather than queue at the gate.
