# EV chilled vans on the road: police checkpost, cut seal, dry ice top-up at a toll plaza

Cab card for the Pune EV chilled van pool. It covers what to do at a police
checkpost when the seal is cut, and topping up with dry ice between depots.

## Police checkpost: the seal was cut

If an officer cuts the seal at a checkpost, put a numbered tamper tape from the
glovebox across the door and drive on. A replacement seal is fitted when the
van gets back to the hub. Write the officer's name on the run sheet.

## Dry ice top-up between depots

A body warming between depots may be topped up with dry ice at a toll plaza or
a listed rest stop, with the EV desk's approval by phone.

## Charging

Charge only at listed chargers, and photograph the charging receipt.
