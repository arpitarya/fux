# Bovigen breeding centre store: the frozen semen room

On the door of the locked room at the store we run for Bovigen's breeding centre near Baramati.

## Who may enter

Only staff on the trained list on this door enter the room. Face shield and cryogenic gloves are worn by everyone inside, without exception. The room's extractor fan is switched on before entry and stays on.

## The containers

Bovigen's frozen straws are stored in six large cryogenic containers on the floor of the room, each on its own wheeled base. They are never lifted, tipped or moved while full.

## Filling

Each storage dewar is filled every 7 days by Bovigen's gas supplier, on Tuesdays. The level in each is measured with the dipstick every morning and written on the sheet beside it. The level must never be below 50 per cent of capacity; if it is, the supplier is called the same day and Bovigen is told.

## Taking doses out

Doses are taken out only by Bovigen's own technicians, into their transport containers, in the room. Our staff do not handle canisters.

## Alarms

The oxygen alarm on the wall sounds if the air in the room becomes unsafe. If it sounds, everyone leaves, shuts the door and calls the store manager. Nobody goes back in until the alarm has stopped and the room has been ventilated.

## Records

Level sheets are kept for three years.
