# Cold Chain Training Plan 2026

Planned for 214 staff across the three DCs. Every module is recorded with attendance, scenario, trainer and the questions staff raised, as clause 10.3 of the [Temperature Excursion Response SOP](01-sop-temperature-excursion.md) requires.

| module | who | length | month |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 Excursion basics | all cold-chain staff | 3 hours | January |
| M2 Night pharma dispatch | Fleet controllers, Nagpur shift leads | 2 hours | February |
| M3 Quarantine release | QA officers, DC managers | 90 minutes | February |
| M4 Washdown | wash bay staff, drivers on dairy rotation | 1 hour | May |
| M5 Lane scoring | Fleet planners | 2 hours | March |
| M6 Site language | new joiners | 30 minutes | first week |

## Module notes

- **M2** walks through [the pink card](30-pharma-night-dispatch-checklist.md) on a real vehicle at the gate. A controller may not dispatch a night pharma run alone until Col. Sandhu or the Fleet head's deputy has signed the practical.
- **M3** covers how stock leaves quarantine under [the green binder](31-quarantine-release-and-return-procedure.md), including when the quarantine dwell limit forces an escalation. Written test, pass mark 80%. Drivers do not attend M3; they never release stock.
- **M4** uses [the Sunday sheet](32-reefer-washdown-and-sanitation-procedure.md). Each trainee times one full CCT with a stopwatch on a real body before signing off.
- **M5** shows planners how each factor of the lane risk score is marked and why dead zones are scored at all.
- **M6** gives every new joiner [the house word list](33-cold-chain-glossary.md) in their first week and 30 minutes with a shift lead to go through it.

## Languages

Hindi and Marathi at Nagpur, Assamese and Hindi at Guwahati, Tamil at Coimbatore. English slides are available but are not a substitute for a trainer in the local language.
