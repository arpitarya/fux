# Seal and label card, stores counter

Hangs at the Stores counter where seal books and label rolls are handed over.

## Fitting at the gate

1. Take the next seal in number order; never skip a number.
2. Bolt seal on owned reefers, cable seal on hired trucks.
3. Record the number on the dispatch note and in QCL-F-35 before the vehicle moves.
4. Photograph the seal with the dock clock in frame.

## Codes

| item | prefix |
|---|---|
| bolt seal | QB |
| cable seal | QC |
| pharma tamper label | QL |

## Books

- A seal book holds 50 seals in number order.
- Two books may be open at the gate, one for each lane.
