# Cold room mapping study TMS-43

Study **TMS-43** is the temperature mapping of cold room G-COLD-1, the vaccine room at Guwahati DC, run over 72 hours from 13 to 16 January 2025. Studies in this series are numbered one after another and the numbers differ by one digit. A study number names one room on one date, never a room in general, so quote the full number.

## 1. Scope

1.1. Empty-room run for 24 hours, then loaded-room run for 48 hours with 10 pallets of water-filled dummy cartons in the vaccine positions.

1.2. Acceptance: every probe inside +2.0 C to +8.0 C for the whole run, and the room back inside limits within 15 minutes of a 3-minute door opening.

## 2. Probe grid

2.1. 24 probes at three heights (low 0.3 m, mid 1.5 m, high 2.7 m) across racks A to D, bays 1 and 2. A position is named rack, bay and height, e.g. D1-low.

2.2. Two reference loggers: one at the evaporator return, one at the door.

## 3. Results

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| hottest position | C2-high, beside the door, max +6.4 C |
| coldest position | D1-low, against the outer north wall, min +2.1 C |
| MKT, whole room, loaded run | +4.2 C |
| recovery after 3-minute door opening | 14 minutes |

3.1. The WCP is D1-low, on the cold side, 0.1 C above the low limit.

## 4. Conclusion

4.1. G-COLD-1 passed with one condition.

4.2. Until a heater interlock is fitted to the room, the Guwahati night lead reads D1-low every two hours between 00:00 and 06:00, from 1 December to the last day of February. Outside those months the reading is not required.

4.3. This study was run in winter only. A summer run is owed before 30 June 2025 and TMS-43 does not cover it.

## 5. Requalification

5.1. G-COLD-1 is re-mapped within 24 months, or sooner after any change to the door, curtain, evaporator or racking.
