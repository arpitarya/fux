# Rate card and surcharges

Author: Anjali Deshmukh  
Applies to standard customer support quotations unless a signed annex says
otherwise.

## Base rates

| service | 2024 rate | 2026 rate | unit |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Vaccine or insulin, Pune to Nagpur lane | Rs. 18.50 | Rs. 21.40 | per kg |
| Dairy crates inside Nagpur city | Rs. 2.80 | Rs. 3.25 | per crate |
| Frozen food intercity pallet | Rs. 1,950 | Rs. 2,240 | per pallet |
| Emergency dry-ice packing | Rs. 38 | Rs. 44 | per kg |

## Surcharges

| item | 2024 rule | 2026 rule |
|---|---|---|
| Diesel surcharge | 5.00% when HSD exceeds Rs. 88/litre | 7.50% when HSD exceeds Rs. 92/litre |
| After-hours dock wait | first 60 minutes free, then Rs. 500/hour | first 45 minutes free, then Rs. 650/hour |
| Pharma audit packet | included for vaccines | included for vaccines and insulin |

GST at 18% is charged on freight, surcharge, and wait time unless the customer
has a written exemption letter.

* Footnote A: From 1 June 2026, the diesel surcharge for standard contracts is
6.25% when HSD exceeds Rs. 92/litre. The table above was not updated before
this note was sent.
* Footnote B: BharatVac and Manjara Dairy use customer annex pricing; do not
quote this card without checking Finance.
