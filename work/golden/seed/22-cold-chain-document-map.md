# Cold chain document map

One page that says **which document to open**, written after a trainer spent a
night shift answering questions that all had the same answer: read the right
file. This map deliberately carries **no** temperature, duration, price, grace
period or hours figure. If you find a number in it, it has been added by mistake
and the linked document wins.

## ⚠ One row this review did not update

**The customer notification row above is deliberately blank.** At the time of
this review two customer notification matrices were on file and the reviewer
could not confirm which one Customer Service had been issued. Do not guess from
this map: open the matrices themselves, take the one with the later effective
date, and check whether it amends the SOP clause rather than merely repeating
it. This row will be filled at the next review of this map.

## What this map is not

It is not an index of everything Quillfern writes. It covers cold chain
operations at Nagpur, Guwahati and Coimbatore. Finance annexes, HR files,
customer contracts and maintenance tickets are not listed here, and their
absence from this page means nothing about whether they exist.
