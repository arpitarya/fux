# Moshi feed testing room: counting bacteria in feed samples

For the small testing room at the Moshi feed warehouse, where we check the dry animal feed and premixes we store for two feed companies.

## What we count

Dry feed carries far fewer bacteria than fresh food, and the feed companies want a quick screen rather than a full analysis. We count total bacteria and moulds on every lot received.

## Plates

The diluted sample is spread on plate count agar and the plates are kept at 35 C for 48 hours before they are read. Mould plates follow their own method in the room's folder.

## The warm cabinet

The room has one incubator, checked once a day at 09:00 with the reference thermometer clipped inside its door. A reading more than 2 C away from the setting is reported to the warehouse QA officer, and plates already inside are read early and marked on the result sheet.

## Media

Plates are bought ready-poured from the supplier, with a certificate for each batch. We do not pour our own.

## Results

Results go to the warehouse QA officer by e-mail the day they are read. A lot whose count is above the feed company's limit is held, and the feed company decides what happens to it.

## Outside testing

Anything beyond a count, such as salmonella or toxins, goes to an accredited outside laboratory on the feed company's list.

## Cleaning

Benches are wiped with disinfectant at the start and end of every day. Used plates go into the yellow bag for the waste contractor.
