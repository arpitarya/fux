SILIGURI CAGE: LOGGER ON THE SHELF - WHEN THE BATTERY GETS CHANGED

A USB logger sitting on the cage shelf has its battery changed every 12 months,
whatever its sticker says.

MINIMUM UNCONDITIONED BLUE-CAP PACKS

The cage keeps at least 30 unconditioned blue-cap packs at all times, which is
enough to remake two shippers.

Count both items every Friday.
