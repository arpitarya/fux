# Year-end visit by Kothari & Bhave: what the statutory auditors will ask for

For Finance staff who will be the contact for Kothari & Bhave, our statutory auditors, during their interim and year-end visits.

## How they work

The audit team walks through each process first, with the person who runs it, and then picks items to check that the controls in the process worked. They tell us which items they want on the morning of each day, not in advance.

## How many items

Their firm's method sets the numbers, and they do not negotiate them. For a control that runs monthly, their attribute test uses 2 items picked from the year; for a control that runs daily or with every transaction, 25 items; for a control that runs once a year, the single occurrence. If one item fails, they extend the test or report the control as not working.

Their sampling is done by the audit software from listings we provide. Make sure every listing you hand over is complete and carries its control total, because they will check that before they pick anything.

## What to have ready

Bank reconciliations for every month, signed; the fixed asset register; the purchase and sales ledgers; payroll summaries; and the minutes of every board meeting. Give them copies, never originals, and keep a list of every document handed over.

## Questions

If you are asked something you cannot answer, say so and find out; do not guess. Every query and its answer is logged in the shared query tracker by the end of the day.

## Their working space

They use the small meeting room on the second floor at head office for the whole visit, with two network points and a printer.
