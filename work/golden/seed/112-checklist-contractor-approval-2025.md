# Contractor approval checklist, 2025 site openings

One page used for the contractors at the sites opened in 2025.

- [ ] Public liability insurance of at least Rs. 25 lakh, for every contractor.
- [ ] Police verification for technicians in pharma areas, renewed every 5 years.
- [ ] Site induction done. The induction card is valid for 24 months at that site only.
- [ ] Emergency attendance within 6 hours for plant faults.
- [ ] Invoices paid within 60 days.
- [ ] Annual review: a score below 60 means probation.
