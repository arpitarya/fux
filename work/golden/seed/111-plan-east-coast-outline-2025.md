# East Coast region: outline plan for site selection

Prepared before the Visakhapatnam plot was chosen, to size the investment for
the board's first look.

- First customer stock: September 2026.
- Chilled pallet positions: 2,000. Frozen pallet positions: 1,200.
- Staff at go-live: 58.
- Owned reefers: 10.

## Bhubaneswar satellite

- Cross-dock only. No pallet stays more than 48 hours.

## Storm season

- Diesel on site for 72 hours at full load from 1 October.
