Pithampur depot dock leveller contractors: public liability insurance, induction card validity, emergency response time, hot work permit, payment days

For the firms that service the dock levellers and dock doors at the Pithampur depot. This note covers public liability insurance, induction card validity, emergency response time, the hot work permit and payment days.

Public liability insurance

Each contractor holds public liability insurance of at least Rs. 25 lakh for any one occurrence.

Induction card validity

A technician's induction card is valid for 6 months.

Emergency response time

For a leveller stuck with a vehicle on the dock, the contractor is on site within 2 hours.

Hot work permit

A hot work permit for welding on a leveller is valid for 24 hours.

Payment days

Accepted invoices are paid within 30 days.
