# PVF-200 portable freezer card: door ajar alarm, full service visit, lifting with stock, mains failure

Taped inside the lid of every PVF-200 portable freezer the engineering pool
lends out.

## Door ajar alarm

If the door is left ajar, the PVF-200 beeps after 60 seconds and rings the
on-call engineer after 5 minutes.

## Full service visit

A full service visit is due every 500 compressor hours or every 3 months,
whichever comes first.

## Lifting with stock inside

A PVF-200 may be lifted by forklift with stock still inside, as long as the
payload is under 150 kg and the straps are fitted.

## Mains failure

On a mains failure the backup genset takes over through the changeover switch
within 60 seconds. Test the changeover at every service visit.
