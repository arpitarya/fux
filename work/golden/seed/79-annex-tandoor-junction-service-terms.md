# Tandoor Junction annex: restaurant freezer failure, dry ice quantity billed, damaged stock reports

Annex to the Tandoor Junction restaurant chain agreement.

## When a restaurant freezer fails: dry ice quantity billed

When a Tandoor Junction restaurant's freezer fails, we pack dry ice for it. The
quantity billed is at least 5 kg per request, whatever smaller quantity the
restaurant asks for.

## Damaged stock reports

The client reports damaged stock within 7 days of delivery, with photographs.
Reports after 7 days are not logged.

## Arrival times

ETA updates are given rounded to 15 minutes.
