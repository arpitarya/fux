# Ammonia alarm at Nagpur: what to do

## When the site siren sounds

1. Stop forklifts and trucks where they are and switch the engines off. Nobody drives through the plant yard.
2. Leave by the nearest door that does not face the compressor house. The security guard brings the gate register.
3. Go to muster point M2 by gate 2, beside the windsock.
4. The shift lead counts heads against the gate register and the dock sheet within 10 minutes and phones the count to the plant operator on extension 215.
5. Nobody goes back for stock, a phone or a truck. Pallets left on a dock are dealt with afterwards under QCL-QA-SOP-17; product loss is a QA matter.

## The compressor house

6. Only the plant crew enter the compressor house, wearing the breathing sets kept in the plant room.

## Going back

7. Stay at M2 until the plant operator gives the all clear in person or over the PA; a text message is not an all clear.
8. Drivers return to their vehicles only after the shift lead has re-counted at M2.
