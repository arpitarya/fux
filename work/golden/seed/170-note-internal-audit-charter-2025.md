# Internal Audit: interim charter and working rules

Issued when the Internal Audit team was set up, to cover its first months until the first annual programme was approved.

## Remit

The team reports to the board on audit matters and to the Chief Financial Officer for administration. It runs no process and signs nothing for another department.

## Items to test

For attribute testing: 15 items for a monthly control and 25 for a daily or transactional one. The sampling method is recorded in the working papers.

## Findings

Findings are graded high, medium or low. For a finding of high severity the auditee responds within 15 working days.

## Staffing

Nobody audits a function they worked in during the previous 12 months. Engagement leads change after five consecutive audits of the same area; rotation is planned by the head of the team. Threats to independence go to the board chair.

## Cash floats

Each site's petty cash imprest is Rs. 10,000. Internal Audit counts it once a year by surprise. Shortages above Rs. 1,000 are reported to Finance.

## Data tests

A duplicate payment test is run each quarter on supplier payments. A Benford test on expense claims is run once a year.

## Reports

The final report is issued within 30 working days of the exit meeting and goes to the department head, the Chief Financial Officer and the board's audit committee.
