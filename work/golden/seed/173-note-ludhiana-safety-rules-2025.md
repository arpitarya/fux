# Ludhiana DC safety rules (opening issue)

Issued for the opening of Ludhiana DC.

## Collapse

The site has two defibrillator units, at the gate cabin and in the canteen. A first aider checks each aed once a month.

## Height

Wear a harness with a lanyard when working at height. Harnesses are withdrawn after seven years.

## Injuries

An lti is notified to the factories inspectorate. Notice within 48 hours of the injury. The investigation report is due within 14 days.

## Freezers

Work in the frozen block for up to 60 minutes, then a 15-minute rewarming break. Report signs of hypothermia to the shift lead.

## Noise

Earmuffs where the survey reads 90 or more on the decibel scale. Hearing tests every 24 months.

## Yard

The pedestrian walkway is 0.9 metres wide. Yard speed limit 15 km/h.

## Fire

Assembly point by the weighbridge. Drills every three months.
