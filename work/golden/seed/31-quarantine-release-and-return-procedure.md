# Quarantine Stock Release and Return Procedure

How stock leaves quarantine bays Q-3 (Nagpur), Q-G2 (Guwahati) and Q-C1 (Coimbatore) after QA has recorded a disposition under clause 6.5 of the [Temperature Excursion Response SOP](01-sop-temperature-excursion.md). Destroyed stock does not leave by this procedure; section 7 of that SOP governs it.

## 1. Release

1.1. Release is recorded on form QCL-F-31 and signed by the QA investigator and a second QA officer who did not investigate the case.

1.2. A white RELEASED tag is fixed beside the red hold tag. The red hold tag is never removed; both stay on the stock until it leaves the site.

1.3. Stock is not released between 22:00 and 06:00, whatever the dispatch plan says. The earliest release of the day is at 06:00.

1.4. Released quantity is counted twice by two people and entered separately from any destroyed quantity.

## 2. Conditional release of pharma stock

2.1. Before a conditional release QA calculates the Stability Budget Remaining (SBR): the minutes above +8.0 C that the customer's stability letter allows for the lot, minus the minutes already used in this and every earlier excursion on the same lot.

2.2. A lot with an SBR below 30 minutes is not conditionally released. It is held pending external decision.

2.3. The SBR and the letter reference are written on QCL-F-31.

## 3. How long stock may sit

3.1. Quarantine Dwell Limit (QDL) is the time stock may stay in quarantine after the excursion is confirmed before the account manager must escalate to the customer's quality head.

| product class | QDL |
|---|---|
| pharma critical | 72 hours |
| dairy | 12 hours |
| frozen food | 48 hours |

3.2. Reaching the QDL does not release anything. It starts an escalation; the stock stays where it is.

## 4. Return to the customer

4.1. Returned stock travels on return challan QCL-F-33, with the seal number written on it.

4.2. If the stock arrived with a customer logger, the same logger travels back with it.

4.3. A DC manager may arrange the vehicle for a return, but may not sign QCL-F-31.
