# Cold room mapping study TMS-45

Study **TMS-45** is the temperature mapping of CBE-FROZEN, the freezer at Coimbatore DC, run over 96 hours from 13 to 17 April 2026, in the weeks when the roof units work hardest. Every logger was checked at the Nagpur bench beforehand and travelled down in one sealed case.

## 1. Scope

1.1. Empty-room run for 24 hours, then loaded-room run for 72 hours with 16 pallets of pre-frozen, water-filled dummy cartons in the stock positions. Two defrost cycles fell inside the loaded run and were left in the record.

1.2. Acceptance: every probe at or colder than the storage limit in clause 2.4 of QCL-QA-SOP-17 for the whole run, defrost included, and the room back at that limit within 20 minutes of a 2-minute door opening.

## 2. Probe grid

2.1. 30 probes at three heights (low 0.2 m, mid 1.2 m, high 2.4 m) across racks A to E, bays 1 and 2. A position is written as A2-high.

2.2. Three reference loggers: one at the return air of each roof unit and one at the door.

## 3. Results

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| warmest position | A1-high, just inside the door, -18.9 C during the second defrost |
| coldest position | E2-low, under the north roof unit, -25.7 C |
| mean air temperature, whole room, loaded run | -21.8 C |
| recovery after 2-minute door opening | 17 minutes |

3.1. The worst case probe (WCP) is A1-high, on the warm side. It came within one degree of the limit only during defrost; outside defrost it never rose above -20.6 C.

## 4. Conclusion

4.1. CBE-FROZEN passed.

4.2. From March to May the top level of rack A carries only outer cartons and empty pallets, never ice cream or any product that softens first.

4.3. The permanent room probe moves to A1-high.

4.4. The second-defrost peak sat close enough to the limit that the roof-unit contractor is asked to check the defrost termination setting at the next service, and to send the setting found and the setting left in writing.
