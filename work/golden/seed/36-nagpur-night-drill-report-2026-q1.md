# Nagpur Night Drill Report, Q1 2026

The quarterly Nagpur night drill required by clause 10.2 of the [Temperature Excursion Response SOP](01-sop-temperature-excursion.md) was run on the night of 17 February 2026, starting at 01:30. 11 staff took part. Trainer: Revathi Iyer.

## Scenario

A simulated high alarm in C2, with the Tessaline export deliberately withheld for the first 40 minutes so the team had to work without the electronic record. At the same time a milk crate was burst in the rear compartment of RF-118.

## Timeline

| time | event |
|---|---|
| 01:30 | C2 alarm simulated |
| 01:41 | Compliance-Red called by the shift lead |
| 02:05 | two vaccine pallets in Q-3 as warm pallets |
| 02:10 | RF-118 rear compartment taken out of use for washdown under [the Sunday sheet](32-reefer-washdown-and-sanitation-procedure.md) |
| 02:20 | USB logger retrieved; export still withheld |
| 06:10 | drill pallets returned to C2 under [the green binder](31-quarantine-release-and-return-procedure.md) |

## What the door sensor showed

The shift's Door Open Minutes (DOM) for C2 were 96, the highest recorded since Tessaline went live, because loading continued during the drill.

## Findings

1. One shift lead emailed the old qa-shared mailbox before calling Compliance-Red. The obsolete habit is named here, as clause 10.4 of the SOP requires: **qa-shared is not an escalation route.**
2. The red sleeve on RF-118 had no spare logger.
3. The night controller paused his run through [the pink card](30-pharma-night-dispatch-checklist.md) when the alarm sounded; no vehicle left during the drill.
4. Two staff asked what "warm pallet" means. It is defined in [the jargon sheet](33-cold-chain-glossary.md).
