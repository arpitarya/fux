# Cold room mapping study TMS-46

Study **TMS-46** is the temperature mapping of CBE-PHARMA, the insulin room at Coimbatore DC, run over 72 hours from 4 to 7 May 2026. The loggers came from the Nagpur bench in the same sealed case used for TMS-45.

## 1. Scope

1.1. Empty-room run for 24 hours, then loaded-room run for 48 hours with 7 pallets of water-filled dummy cartons in the insulin positions.

1.2. Sixteen loggers stood at four heights (0.2 m, 0.9 m, 1.6 m and 2.3 m) on racks A and B, bays 1 and 2, with one reference logger at the evaporator return and one at the door. Positions are named rack, bay and height, e.g. B1-2.3.

1.3. Acceptance: every logger inside the insulin range in clause 2.2 of QCL-QA-SOP-17 for the whole run, and the room back inside range within 12 minutes of a 2-minute door opening.

## 2. Results

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| hottest position | B1-2.3, beside the door and under the roof-unit drain line, max +7.2 C |
| coldest position | A2-0.2, in the evaporator discharge, min +2.8 C |
| MKT (mean kinetic temperature), whole room, loaded run | +4.9 C |
| recovery after 2-minute door opening | 10 minutes |

2.1. The WCP is B1-2.3, on the warm side, 0.8 C under the high limit. Its afternoon readings ran about half a degree above its morning readings on all three days.

## 3. Conclusion

3.1. CBE-PHARMA passed.

3.2. From March to May no insulin carton is placed on the top shelf of rack B, bay 1; that shelf carries only empty totes.

3.3. The permanent room probe moves to B1-2.3.

## 4. Requalification

4.1. CBE-PHARMA is re-mapped between March and May 2028, so that the next run again falls in the hot months, or sooner if a roof unit is replaced or the racks are moved.
