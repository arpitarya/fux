# Vadodara bonded store card: mock recall, returned goods back to stock, batch record retention

Pinned at the Vadodara QA desk. This card covers three things: the mock recall,
when returned goods may go back to stock, and how long batch records are kept.

## Mock recall

A mock recall is run once a year, in November, on a batch the QA lead picks.
Every unit of the chosen batch must be traced, on the shelf, on a vehicle or at
a consignee, within 8 hours of the exercise starting. The result goes to the
batch owner.

## Returned goods back to stock

Returned medicines may go back into saleable stock if they come back within
7 days of delivery, the outer cartons are unopened and the shift QA officer
signs the return note. Anything else is held for the owner's decision.

## Batch record retention

Batch records, meaning goods-in sheets, dispatch notes and logger files, are
kept for five years after the expiry date of the batch, and then shredded on
site.

## Deviation numbers

Deviation numbers run in one series for the store and are never reused.
