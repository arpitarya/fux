# Ludhiana DC chambers: handover note

Written at the end of the first Ludhiana build to record the tests the turnkey contractor ran before handing the chambers over. The figures are the contractor's defaults.

## Pipework

Pipework charged with dry nitrogen. Pressure held for 12 hours. Each brazed joint inspected by the contractor.

## Evacuation

System pumped to 1,000 micron under vacuum and held below 1,500 for 15 minutes.

## Cooling down

Freezers brought down below 0 C at up to 4 C per hour. The pulldown gradient was logged every four hours.

## Envelope survey

Infrared survey after pull-down. A joint more than 5 C colder than the panel beside it is resealed. Thermography images saved by the contractor.

## Outstanding items

The contractor's snag list graded A and B. B items to be closed within 45 days of completion; the punch list is kept by the contractor.

## Sign-off

Each test witnessed by the contractor's engineer. One signature, the contractor's, on each test sheet. No other signatories were required.
