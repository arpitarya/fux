# Ludhiana DC shift timings and staff bus routes

## Shift timings

| shift | timing |
|---|---|
| morning | 06:00 to 14:30 |
| evening | 14:00 to 22:30 |
| office | 09:30 to 18:00 |

The half-hour overlap is for the handover.

## Staff bus routes

Two buses run for each shift change, free for staff and agency workers.

| route | first pick-up | for the morning shift | for the evening shift |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Bharat Nagar Chowk | 05:15 | 13:15 |
| B | Jalandhar bypass, near the bus stand | 05:20 | 13:20 |

Return buses leave the gate at 14:40 and 22:40, stopping at the same points in reverse.

## Missed bus

Staff who miss the return bus because their shift ran late are given a taxi paid by the company; ask the gate cabin.
