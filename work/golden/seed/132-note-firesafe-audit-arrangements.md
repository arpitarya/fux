# Fire safety audits by Firesafe Consultants: arrangements for site managers

Firesafe Consultants carry out the yearly fire safety audit at each Quillfern site under their framework contract. This note tells site managers what to expect.

## Before the visit

Firesafe give at least two weeks' notice of the date. The site manager makes sure the fire extinguisher service tags, the alarm test log, the emergency lighting log and the last drill report are ready in one folder.

## On the day

The auditor walks the whole site with the site manager or a shift lead, including the roof, the plant room and the yard. Expect questions to staff at random about the alarm, the assembly point and the nearest extinguisher.

## Closing the visit

The visit ends with an exit meeting in which the auditor goes through every observation with the site manager. Firesafe then send their written report within 10 working days of that meeting, to the site manager and to the head of health and safety, with a copy for the site's safety committee to read at its next sitting.

## Answering

The site manager answers every observation within 30 days with an action, an owner and a date. Answers go to the head of health and safety, who sends them to Firesafe.

## Costs

The audit is paid centrally under the framework contract. Repairs found necessary are paid by the site.
