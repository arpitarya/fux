# Cold room mapping study TMS-41

Study **TMS-41** is the temperature mapping of cold room C2, the vaccine room at Nagpur DC, run over 72 hours from 6 to 9 May 2024. Studies in this series are numbered one after another and the numbers differ by one digit. A study number names one room on one date, never a room in general, so quote the full number.

## 1. Scope

1.1. Empty-room run for 24 hours, then loaded-room run for 48 hours with 14 pallets of water-filled dummy cartons in the vaccine positions.

1.2. Acceptance: every probe inside +2.0 C to +8.0 C for the whole run, and the room back inside limits within 15 minutes of a 3-minute door opening.

## 2. Probe grid

2.1. 27 probes at three heights (low 0.3 m, mid 1.5 m, high 2.7 m) across racks A, B and C, bays 1 to 3. A position is named rack, bay and height, e.g. A1-high.

2.2. Two reference loggers: one at the evaporator return, one at the door.

## 3. Results

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| hottest position | A1-high, beside the door curtain, max +7.6 C |
| coldest position | C3-low, under the evaporator, min +2.4 C |
| Mean Kinetic Temperature (MKT), whole room, loaded run | +5.1 C |
| recovery after 3-minute door opening | 11 minutes |

3.1. MKT is reported because customer stability letters are written against it, not against the arithmetic average.

## 4. Conclusion

4.1. C2 passed.

4.2. A1-high is the room's weak point. During vaccine season no vaccine pallet is stored on the top level of rack A, and the top level of rack A carries only empty totes.

4.3. The permanent room probe stays at the centre position B2-mid.

## 5. Requalification

5.1. C2 is re-mapped within 24 months, or sooner after any change to the door, curtain, evaporator or racking.
