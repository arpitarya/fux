# Hosur depot card: handheld thermometer checks, dry ice totes, CO2 monitor over the chest

For the Hosur depot in Tamil Nadu. This card covers how often handheld
thermometers are checked, when a dry ice tote needs two people, and the CO2
monitor over the dry ice chest.

## Handheld thermometer checks

Every handheld thermometer in daily use at Hosur is checked against the
certified thermometer every 7 days, in a stirred jug of ice and water. The two
readings must agree within 0.5 C.

## Dry ice totes: when it takes two people

Any dry ice tote above 12 kg is carried by two people.

## CO2 monitor over the dry ice chest

A CO2 monitor is fitted over the dry ice chest. It beeps at 5,000 ppm. If it
beeps, leave the store and call the shift lead.
