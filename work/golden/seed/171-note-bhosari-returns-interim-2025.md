# Bhosari hub: interim rules for returned goods

Interim rules for the first months of the Bhosari hub, until the returns manual is issued.

## Return numbers

Customer service issues an rma for every return. The number is valid for 28 days. Goods without an authorisation number are refused at the gate.

## Sorting

Every line is judged within 48 hours. Grading puts each line in one bucket: A restock, B owner decides, C destroy.

## Chilled returns

Chilled returns may ride on a delivering vehicle's backhaul. The vehicle must reach the hub within 10 hours of collection. Consolidation of several customers' returns is arranged by Fleet Control.

## Debit notes

Customer service completes the reconciliation of each debit note with the hub's records within 10 working days. Any discrepancy is recorded; a difference above 5 per cent goes to the account manager.

## Destruction

Pharmaceutical stock leaves for destruction with the contractor's manifest. Incineration must take place within 60 days of collection.

## Withdrawn batches

Recalled stock is held in bay R-2 under strict segregation and counted weekly. If the customer has given no instruction after 60 days, the account manager writes to the customer.

## Hours

The hub is open 08:00 to 20:00, Monday to Saturday.
