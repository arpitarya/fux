# Reefer asset file RF-119

Asset **RF-119** is an owned chilled reefer based at Guwahati DC. It was bought
in the same 2021 batch as [RF-118](16-reefer-RF-118-asset-file.md) but it is a
smaller single-compartment body and it is not a pharma asset. Fleet Control has
twice received a Nagpur pharma booking on RF-119 that was meant for RF-118, so
the two files are kept separate and neither one is a copy of the other.

## What RF-119 may carry

RF-119 runs the Guwahati dairy and chilled-grocery circuit. Milk and paneer are
its normal load.

RF-119 may carry vaccines or insulin **only** inside a validated passive
shipper, because the body has no cradle and no second compartment to separate
pharma from dairy stock. A passive shipper is hired per trip and the charge is a
line on [the H2 2026 rate card](12-rate-card-2026-h2.md); Fleet Control raises
the hire request, not the customer service agent. Without a shipper, RF-119 is
refused for pharma critical stock whatever the urgency, and
[the Driver Hours and Road Safety Policy](08-driver-hours-and-safety-policy.md)
clause 1.3 is the reason no manager may overrule that at the gate.

The temperature limits for whatever RF-119 carries are set by
[the Temperature Excursion Response SOP](01-sop-temperature-excursion.md). This
file sets none.

## Monsoon working

RF-119 works Guwahati through the monsoon. Rain cover over rear pallets is
mandatory during monsoon unloading, and since June 2026 the handling is billed
to the customer as a line on [the rate card](12-rate-card-2026-h2.md) rather
than absorbed. The yard rule about not idling inside the small yard after 21:30
is in [Dock scheduling rules 2026](13-dock-scheduling-rules-2026.md).

On 18 April 2026 RF-119 reached Guwahati without a rain cover over the rear
pallets and the night team reported it to Riniki Bora by phone. No stock was
lost. Stores now keeps two spare covers in the RF-119 cab locker.

## Slot booking

RF-119 slots are booked in Dockwise. Guwahati moved off phone booking on
1 February 2026 under [PROJ-123](19-project-PROJ-123-dockwise-rollout.md);
Riniki Bora still confirms night slots by phone when the VPN is down and the
slot is written on the guard register as before.

## Servicing

Service is every 500 engine hours or six months, whichever comes first. The last
service was 3 February 2026 at the Guwahati contract garage. RF-119 has no
Nagpur service history because it has never been based there.
