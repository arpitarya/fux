# Ammonia alarm at Nagpur: why M2, and why nobody goes back

## Why M2 and not the canteen lawn

Until 2024 the ammonia muster point was M1, the canteen lawn. In the July 2024 monsoon drill the windsock put the canteen lawn downwind of the compressor house for four of the six drill hours.
M2, by gate 2, sits upwind under the south-west monsoon wind and has a second
way out on to the ring road. M1 is still the fire muster point.

## Why nobody goes back

Nobody goes back for stock because in the 2024 drill a loader went back to dock 5 for his phone and was counted missing for 12 minutes.
A missing person turns an evacuation into a search, and a search sends the plant
crew into the wrong place.

## Why the first stage is at 25 ppm

25 ppm leaves room to act before the 35 ppm short-term exposure limit is reached anywhere a person stands.
A higher first stage would give the plant crew less time and the docks more
exposure.

## What the drill also showed

Dock 5 is the nearest shutter to the compressor house, and it was the slowest
dock to empty. Several phones rang at once during the count and drowned out the
names, so nobody could tell at first who was missing. One trolley was left across
the dock 5 walkway, and people had to go back round it. The count is now taken
against the register and not by shouting.
