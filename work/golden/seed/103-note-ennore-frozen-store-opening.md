# Ennore frozen store opening: first stock date, frozen pallet positions, staff at go-live, owned reefers, capex and payback

For the Ennore frozen store north of Chennai. This note covers the first stock
date, frozen pallet positions, staff at go-live, owned reefers, and capex and
payback.

## First stock date

The store receives its first customer stock on 15 October 2026.

## Frozen pallet positions

The frozen chamber holds 2,200 pallet positions.

## Staff at go-live

The store opens with 48 staff on the payroll.

## Owned reefers

The store starts with 10 owned reefers.

## Capex and payback

Capex is Rs. 28 crore, with payback in month 20 after first stock.
