# Probe and logger calibration at the QA bench

Carried out at the QA bench in Nagpur by any QA officer or trained shift lead.
The bench kit is the insulated jug, the ice scoop, the calibration chamber and
the bench master logger.

## Hand-held probes

1. Fill the insulated jug with crushed ice made from potable water. Top it up with cold potable water to just below the ice line and stir for 30 seconds.
2. Hold the probe tip 5 cm deep in the slurry, clear of the jug wall, for 3 minutes before you read the display.
3. The probe passes if it reads within 0.5 C of 0.0 C.
4. Repeat for every hand-held probe on the monthly list. A probe is due every 30 days.

## USB loggers

5. Put the logger beside the bench master in the calibration chamber at +5.0 C for 30 minutes; it passes if it reads within 0.3 C of the master.
6. Every spare logger is checked this way before it is packed into a red sleeve, whatever its sticker says.

## At the bench

7. A pass gets a green sticker with the next due date, and the reading goes on QCL-F-12 the same day.
8. A fail gets red DO NOT USE tape and goes in the blue bin at the QA desk. Do not adjust the offset yourself. QA sends failed instruments to Metrosure Labs, Pune.

## Keeping the bench tidy

Spare sticker rolls live in the top drawer. The date stamp is reset every Monday
by the day QA officer. Green tape marks the clean side of the bench, where
nothing wet is put down. Filled QCL-F-12 sheets go to the QA file room at month
end. The jug is emptied and dried at the end of every shift, and the scoop is
kept in its bag.
