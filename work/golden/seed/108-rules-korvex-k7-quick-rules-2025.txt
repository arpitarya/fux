KORVEX K7 PILOT QUICK RULES (WALUJ, 2025)

These quick rules were written for the twenty-device K7 pilot at the Waluj
workshop.

SCREEN LOCK
An idle K7 locks after 5 minutes.

PIN CHANGE
PINs are six digits and are changed every 180 days.

WRONG ATTEMPTS
Ten wrong attempts lock the user out.

BATTERY
Batteries are retired at 400 charge cycles.

LOST DEVICE
Report a lost K7 the same shift. The desk wipes it after 72 hours.

AGENCY STAFF
Agency staff scan under the shift lead's login.

UNRETURNED DEVICE
A K7 not returned by a leaver is recovered at Rs. 15,000.
