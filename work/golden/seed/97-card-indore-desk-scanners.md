# Indore order desk scanners: screen lock, passcode change, wrong attempts, battery replacement, software update night

Taped to the Indore order desk scanner rack. This card covers the screen lock,
passcode changes, wrong attempts, battery replacement and the software update
night.

## Screen lock

A desk scanner left idle locks its screen after 10 minutes.

## Passcode change

Passcodes are four digits and are changed every 60 days.

## Wrong attempts

Three wrong passcode attempts lock the scanner until the IT desk resets it.

## Battery replacement

A scanner battery is replaced at 300 charge cycles.

## Software update night

Updates are pushed on Sunday night, between 02:00 and 04:00, to scanners left in
the rack.
