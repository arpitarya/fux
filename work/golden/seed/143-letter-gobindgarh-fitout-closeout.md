# Mandi Gobindgarh store fit-out: closing out the contractor

A copy of the terms we have agreed with the fit-out contractor for the small chilled store at Mandi Gobindgarh, kept for the site manager.

## The list

Our engineer keeps one list of everything still wrong with the fit-out, walked with the contractor's site engineer every Friday. Each item is graded A, which stops the store taking stock, or B, which does not.

## Completion

We certify practical completion only when no A item is open. Every B item must then be closed within 14 days of the certificate. The store is small and opens straight into a busy season, so the period is shorter than on our larger builds.

## Closing items

An item is closed when our engineer has seen the repair, never on the contractor's word. The contractor's own punch sheet is not accepted in place of ours.

## Money

Five per cent of the contract value is held back until the last snag is closed and signed. The contractor may ask for part of it to be released early only by a letter to the regional engineer.

## Defects

Faults found after completion are reported in writing and repaired within the contractor's defects period of twelve months.

## Contacts

Our engineer on site, the contractor's site engineer, and the regional engineer for anything the two of them cannot settle between themselves on site. Copies of every letter go to the site manager, who keeps the fit-out file.
