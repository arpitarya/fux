# Changodar pharma store: quality summary for fit-out

Written during the fit-out of the Changodar pharma store so that the first
customers had something to audit against before the quality manual existed.

## Humidity limit in the CRT area

The CRT area is held at or below 60 per cent RH.

## Walk-in chamber requalification

The walk-in chambers are requalified every 24 months.

## Logger gaps at goods-in

The goods-in clerk accepts a gap of up to 15 minutes in an inbound vehicle's
logger record.

## Returns back to stock

Returned medicines go back to stock if they come back within 48 hours of
delivery with an unbroken logger record.

## Mock recall

A mock recall is run once a year. Every unit must be traced within 6 hours.

## Self-inspection

The store inspects itself twice a year.

## Batch records

Batch files are kept for 3 years after the batch's expiry date.
