# Nutrifeed premixes account: how the two companies meet

For our account team on the Nutrifeed account, whose vitamin and mineral premixes for animal feed we store at the Talegaon ambient store and deliver to feed mills.

## Every quarter

The account manager and Nutrifeed's logistics manager hold a review once a quarter, in the second week after the quarter ends. They go through the quarter's figures, returns, damaged bags, open complaints and Nutrifeed's plans for the next quarter. The account manager writes the minutes and sends them within a week.

## Once a year

A steering group of senior managers from both companies meets once a year, in March, before Nutrifeed sets its next year's budget. Our operations head and the account manager attend for us.

## Changing the terms

Any change to the service terms or the measures is agreed only at one of those meetings. Our governance rule on this account is simple: nothing agreed on a phone call counts until it is written into the next set of minutes.

## Between meetings

Day-to-day questions go between the account manager and Nutrifeed's logistics manager by phone and e-mail.

## Escalation

Problems the two of them cannot settle go to our customer service lead and Nutrifeed's supply chain head.

## Records

All minutes are kept in the account file for the length of the contract and three years after.
