# Lane qualification LQ-55

Record **LQ-55** qualifies the insulin lane from Coimbatore DC through the Palakkad gap to customer pharmacies in Thrissur.

## 1. Lane

| field | value |
|---|---|
| origin | Coimbatore DC |
| destination | Thrissur customer pharmacies |
| route | via Walayar and Palakkad |
| distance | 118 km |
| product class | pharma critical, chilled |
| vehicle class | owned_reefer_chilled |
| season profile | south-west monsoon, June to September |
| trial runs | 3, from 1 to 15 July 2026 |

## 2. Score

2.1. LQ-55 scores an LRS of 10 of 25. The Walayar state border check is the only stop of any length, and the winds through the gap bring rain to the Kerala drops rather than heat.

2.2. All three trial runs were day runs, out and back inside one shift.

## 3. Trial results

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| warmest body reading | +5.7 C, run 2, during a 26-minute wait at Walayar |
| longest telemetry gap | 4 minutes, east of Palakkad |
| PCH used | 35 minutes |
| door openings per run | 4, multi-drop |

## 4. Conditions

4.1. PCH 35 minutes before loading at Coimbatore.

4.2. If officers at Walayar open the body, the driver follows section 7 of the Fleet Operations Manual, and Fleet Control tells QA how many minutes the doors stood open.

4.3. Every run carries a USB logger beside the product.

4.4. Rain cover goes over the rear pallets at each Kerala drop while it is raining.

4.5. LQ-55 is valid for the 2026 south-west monsoon season only.
