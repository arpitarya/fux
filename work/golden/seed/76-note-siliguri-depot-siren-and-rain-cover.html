Siliguri depot: fire siren, where people gather, monsoon rain cover

Notice board copy for the Siliguri depot. It covers the fire siren, where people gather, and the rain cover charge.

Fire siren: where people gather

When the fire siren sounds, everyone leaves the building and gathers at the front-gate car park. The shift lead counts heads against the gate register.

Monsoon rain cover

Rain cover over rear pallets is used from June to September and is billed to the customer at Rs. 300 per trip.
