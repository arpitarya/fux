# Cold room mapping study TMS-44

Study **TMS-44** is the temperature mapping of cold room C2, the vaccine room at Nagpur DC, run over 72 hours from 23 to 26 March 2026. It replaces TMS-41 for C2. Studies in this series are numbered one after another and the numbers differ by one digit. A study number names one room on one date, never a room in general, so quote the full number.

The curtain and rail replacement of March 2025 should have triggered a re-map on its own. It did not, and the BharatVac audit of February 2026 found the gap. Since then an air curtain has been fitted over the entrance (November 2025) and rack D was added along the west wall (January 2026).

## 1. Scope

1.1. Empty-room run for 24 hours, then loaded-room run for 48 hours with 18 pallets of water-filled dummy cartons in the vaccine positions. The loaded run replayed the busiest night shift of February 2026, a DOM of 96, with every opening repeated on the same timetable.

1.2. Acceptance: every probe inside +2.0 C to +8.0 C for the whole run, and the room back inside limits within 15 minutes of a 3-minute opening.

## 2. Probe grid

2.1. 36 probes at three heights (low 0.3 m, mid 1.5 m, high 2.7 m) across racks A to D, bays 1 to 3. A position is named rack, bay and height, e.g. D3-high.

2.2. Two reference loggers: one at the evaporator return, one at the entrance.

## 3. Results

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| hottest position | D3-high, against the west wall under the roof, max +7.7 C |
| coldest position | C3-low, under the evaporator, min +2.6 C |
| A1-high, the old weak point | max +6.2 C |
| MKT, whole room, loaded run | +5.4 C |
| recovery after 3-minute opening | 7 minutes |

3.1. The WCP is D3-high, on the warm side, 0.3 C under the high limit.

## 4. Conclusion

4.1. C2 passed.

4.2. The rack A top-level restriction set in TMS-41 is lifted; the air curtain holds that position.

4.3. From April to September the top level of rack D carries only empty totes and outer packaging, never vaccine.

4.4. The permanent room probe TSL-NGP-C2-07 moves from B2-mid to the WCP.

## 5. Requalification

5.1. C2 is re-mapped within 24 months, or sooner after any change to the entrance, curtain, evaporator or racking.
