# Product Testing Laboratory: methods and checks (revision 1)

Issued when the laboratory opened at the Bhosari hub in October 2025.

## Counts

Total viable counts are spread on plate count agar. Plates are read after 48 hours at 35 C. Each incubator is checked once a day.

## Rapid swabs

Rapid atp swabs are used on cleaned surfaces. A reading of 150 rlu or below is a pass.

## Portions kept

An aliquot of every food sample is frozen for retesting. Portions are kept for 30 days after the report.

## Outside comparison

The laboratory joins an external proficiency scheme once a year. No interlaboratory comparison for packaging tests yet.

## Small volumes

Each pipette has a gravimetric check. Checks are every six months.

## Sterilising

Autoclave loads run at 121 C for 20 minutes. A spore strip is run once a month.
