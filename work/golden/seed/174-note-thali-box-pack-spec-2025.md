# Thali Box pilot: pack note

Packaging used for the Thali Box meal-kit pilot from April 2025.

## Outer

Three-ply corrugated slotted box, tested by bct. Compression strength at least 300 kgf.

## Insulation

Moulded eps box, polystyrene walls 20 mm thick.

## Cold source

Each pcm brick conditioned for 24 hours. Three packs of 400 g each per chilled kit.

## Labels

Use-by date on the lid in characters at least 4 mm high. Every shipper carries an sscc label to the gs1 standard.

## Transit test

Kits pass an ista-style test of 6 drops from 45 cm, followed by a doorstep simulation in the chamber.

## Returns

Customers pay a deposit on their first kit. Rs. 50, refunded if the box comes back within 7 days. Shippers that cannot be reused are recyclable.

## Kitting room

Held at +15 C.
