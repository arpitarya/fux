# Gel pack and shipper figures, QA room sheet

Pinned beside conditioning cabinet CC-1.

## Packs

- blue-cap pack — conditioned at +5.0 C, pharma only.
- white-cap pack — conditioned at -20 C, frozen goods only; never mixed with blue caps.
- A pack is retired after 60 cycles.

## Shippers

- 18 blue-cap packs go in each shipper.
- Shipper conditioning time: 18 hours at +5.0 C.
- A closed shipper holds its range for 72 hours.

| item | figure |
|---|---|
| set point | +5.0 C |
| alarm band | +2.0 C to +8.0 C |
