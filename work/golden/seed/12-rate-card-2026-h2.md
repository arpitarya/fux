# Rate card and surcharges, H2 2026

Author: Anjali Deshmukh

This card replaces the earlier card and its footnotes. Footnote A of the old
card is now inside the table where it belongs. Applies to standard customer
support quotations unless a signed annex says otherwise.

## Base rates

| service | rate from 1 June 2026 | unit |
|---|---:|---|
| Vaccine or insulin, Pune to Nagpur lane | Rs. 22.60 | per kg |
| Dairy crates inside Nagpur city | Rs. 3.25 | per crate |
| Frozen food intercity pallet | Rs. 2,240 | per pallet |
| Emergency dry-ice packing | Rs. 49 | per kg |
| Passive shipper hire, pharma | Rs. 1,150 | per shipper per trip |

## Surcharges

| item | rule from 1 June 2026 |
|---|---|
| Diesel surcharge | 6.25% when HSD exceeds Rs. 92/litre |
| After-hours dock wait | first 45 minutes free, then Rs. 650/hour |
| Pharma audit packet | included for vaccines and insulin |
| Monsoon rain-cover handling, Guwahati | Rs. 260 per trip, June to September |

GST at 18% is charged on freight, surcharge, and wait time unless the customer
has a written exemption letter.

* Footnote A: The 7.50% diesel surcharge printed on the earlier card was never
  applied to standard contracts after 1 June 2026. Quote 6.25%.
* Footnote B: BharatVac and Manjara Dairy use customer annex pricing; do not
  quote this card without checking Finance.
* Footnote C: Dry-ice packing moved from Rs. 44 to Rs. 49 per kg because the
  Nagpur supplier revised its slab in April 2026.
