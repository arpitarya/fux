# Hadapsar sales office: the cash box

Kept in the drawer of the office manager's desk at the Hadapsar sales office.

## What it is for

The sales office keeps a small amount of cash for courier charges, auto fares for urgent documents, stationery bought in an emergency and refreshments for visitors. Anything that can wait for a purchase order goes through a purchase order.

## How much

The office's petty cash is kept at Rs. 8,000. It works as an imprest: every voucher spent is replaced from the bank at the end of the month, so the box always returns to the same total.

## Vouchers

Every payment out of the box needs a voucher signed by the person who spent the money and by the office manager, with the bill attached. A payment without a bill needs a written reason.

## Counting

The office manager counts the box every quarter, on the last working day of March, June, September and December, with one other member of staff present. Cash plus vouchers must equal the fixed total. A shortage of more than Rs. 200 is reported to the regional accountant by e-mail the same day.

## Keys

The box and the drawer each have one key, both held by the office manager. When she is on leave, the keys are handed to her deputy against a signature in the key book.
