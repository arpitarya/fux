# Siliguri vaccine relay: passive shipper on a long run, ringing control, flat tyre help

For drivers on the Siliguri vaccine relay into the hill districts.

## Passive shipper on a long run

A closed passive shipper is good for 72 hours from lid closing. Ring control
at hour 60 on the road so that a re-pack can be arranged.

## Gel conditioning before departure

Packs are conditioned for 12 hours before the shipper is made up.

## Flat tyre on a loaded vehicle

The listed tyre vendor should reach a loaded vehicle within 2 hours. Wait with
the vehicle; do not change the tyre yourself.
