# Malerkotla depot: protecting your ears around the plant

For everyone who works at or visits the Malerkotla depot.

## Where it is loud

The plant room, the generator shed and the compressor bay behind dock 2 are loud enough to damage hearing slowly, without anyone noticing until it is too late. The regional safety officer measures the noise in those places every year.

## The line

At Malerkotla, earmuffs are worn by everyone in any area where the yearly survey measured 80 or more on the decibel scale. That line is lower than the legal minimum, because the plant room's noise changes pitch sharply when the compressors cycle, and people found it tiring at levels the law allows.

The signs at the doors show which areas are marked. Dispensers hang beside each door; take a fresh pair if yours are damaged.

## Hearing tests

People who work in marked areas for most of their shift have a hearing test every two years with the occupational health provider. HR arranges the appointment and pays for it.

## Results

Test results are private. If a test shows a change, the provider tells the person first, and the person decides whether to tell their shift lead.

## New machines

When a new machine is installed in a marked area, the safety officer measures again before the machine goes into normal use.
